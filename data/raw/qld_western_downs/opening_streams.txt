The proposal has considered a range of environmental, infrastructure and servicing constraints through the

detailed design process. While the site is largely cleared, existing wetlands and identified habitat values

require careful management to minimise en

vironmental impacts and inform the siting of buildings,

infrastructure and access arrangements.

The site is also affected by existing overhead and underground electricity infrastructure associated with the

adjoining Powerlink transmission network, together with a telecommunications mast located west of the site

along Grahams Road. These constraints h

address infrastructure requirements.
