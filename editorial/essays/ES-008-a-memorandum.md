## The common perception

On 5 December 2025, NEXTDC told the stock exchange it would "Join OpenAI in Australia as an Infrastructure Partner", starting at its planned S7 campus at Eastern Creek in Sydney's west [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025]. By July 2026, a Reuters headline called S7 "OpenAI's Australian data centre" [SRC_REUTERS_S7].

The only primary record of the deal is a memorandum of understanding, and it states no price, term, capacity or stake [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025]. In every NEXTDC report we have read since, OpenAI is not mentioned once [RG-014]. A government fast track and a $7 billion planning application now rest on a partnership the public cannot see [SRC_NXT_1H26_PRESENTATION] [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING].

## What the records show

### What was signed

NEXTDC's announcement says it "has agreed a Memorandum of Understanding" with OpenAI, under which the two "will collaborate on the planning, development and operation of a next-generation hyperscale AI campus and large-scale GPU supercluster" at S7 [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025]. "Subject to securing necessary approvals, the first phase of the S7 project is expected to be delivered in the second half of calendar 2027." [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025]

A memorandum of understanding records an intention to work together [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025]. This one, as announced, names no price, no length of contract, no amount of capacity and no ownership stake [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025].

### A week later

On 12 December 2025, NEXTDC lodged a "Response to ASX Compliance Letter" [SRC_ASX_NXT_ANNOUNCEMENTS_2025]. The exchange's terms of use limit free reading to private investors, so we did not open it [SRC_ASX_NXT_ANNOUNCEMENTS_2025]. This record does not tell you what the exchange asked, or what NEXTDC answered. The response would [RG-014].

### Not mentioned once

We then read what NEXTDC has published since: its half-year results presentation and accounts, its April and May 2026 funding announcements, its full-year results, presentation and annual report, and its S7 web page [RG-014]. None mentions OpenAI [RG-014] [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT] [SRC_NEXTDC_S7]. We tried OpenAI's own website too, and it turned our crawler away [RG-014].

S7 kept moving without the name. In February 2026, NEXTDC reported "S7 granted fast track status under the NSW Government's Investment Delivery Authority (IDA) program" [SRC_NXT_1H26_PRESENTATION]. By August, its results presentation gave S7's initial capacity as "TBD", and said it "continues its evaluation of partnering with third party capital through a JVCo structure for S4, S7 and M5" [SRC_NXT_FY26_PRESENTATION]. Its annual report lists NEXTDC S7 Holdings Pty Ltd, wholly owned at 30 June 2026 [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT].

### How big is S7

NEXTDC's own planning document, its scoping report, gives the size two ways. In one place it says "Power Consumption 612MW"; in another, "The expected total site load is approximately 700MW, with infrastructure designed to support up to 875MVA" [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]. Its results presentation says "~550MW+" [SRC_NXT_FY26_PRESENTATION]. Every one of these is a figure for the whole site, and none says how much would run the computers themselves [RG-099].

The same report puts the investment at $7,154,563,995 and the water demand at 5.44 megalitres a day at about 70 per cent load, rising to 7.79 at full size [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]. No agreement from the water utility is public [RG-017].

## Our view

A headline that calls S7 "OpenAI's" data centre turns a memorandum into a tenancy.

That matters beyond the headline. The State has put S7 on a fast track. The planning system will weigh its power and water. NEXTDC is seeking outside capital for it. Each of them is entitled to know whether the partner in the announcement has committed to anything, and NEXTDC, which made the announcement, is best placed to say.

If the memorandum binds no one to a price, a term or a size, the plain thing is to say so.

> To the investor relations team, NEXTDC Limited: what capacity, term and price does the memorandum of understanding with OpenAI commit either party to? If it commits neither party to any of these, will NEXTDC say so to the market?
