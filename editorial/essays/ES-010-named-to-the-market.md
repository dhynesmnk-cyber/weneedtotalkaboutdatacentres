## The common perception

On 14 September 2026, the ABC reported that "Australia's largest proposed data centre could draw a quarter of the state's peak power" [SRC_ABC_WD]. The campus is planned for a former feedlot site in Queensland's Western Downs [SRC_ABC_WD].

Who it is for is a question the regional council's file does not answer. The application names neither the developer nor the tenant [SRC_WDDP_TPR] [RG-012]. Two days after the ABC's report, the property group Dexus told the stock exchange that the consortium behind the campus had entered into lease documentation with the Australian subsidiary of Anthropic [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026]. The market heard the tenant's name from a party to the deal. The council's file still does not carry it [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026] [SRC_WDDP_TPR].

## What the records show

### The application

The planning report for the Western Downs Digital Park is dated 14 August 2026 and was prepared for WDDP Pty Ltd [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. It went to Western Downs Regional Council by email on 18 and 19 August, runs to about 870 pages with its appendices, and describes "approximately 2,160MW of IT load" [SRC_WDDP_TPR].

It mentions neither Anthropic nor Zerra, the developer the trade press named [SRC_WDDP_TPR] [RG-012]. The national company register shows WDDP Pty Ltd was created on 28 July 2026, seventeen days before the report was dated [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

### The announcement

On 16 September 2026, Dexus released an update on Australian Data Centres, a business in which it holds an 85 per cent interest [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026]. It said that business "holds a 25% interest in a consortium with Zerra DC and Macquarie Capital (Consortium) in connection with the proposed development of a hyperscale data centre campus for an Australian subsidiary of Anthropic in the Western Downs region of Queensland" [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026].

Then: "The Consortium has entered into lease documentation with the Australian subsidiary of Anthropic for the delivery of the first stage of the campus, subject to customary approvals." [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026]

That one sentence is the only primary record of the tenancy we hold [RG-012]. When we searched Anthropic's own news page in October, we found no announcement [RG-012].

### What the sentence leaves out

The release states no lease term, no price and no size for the first stage [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026]. It does not say what "customary approvals" are, or whether the council's own decision is one of them [RG-012].

It does name the developer. Zerra DC, which the trade press reported is owned by an investment firm called AGP, is a member of the consortium [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026] [SRC_DCD_ZERRA]. Who owns WDDP Pty Ltd, the company that applied, is still not on the public record [RG-052].

### The same question, put to everyone

This observatory asks of every data centre who will run it and who it is for. In New South Wales, nine planning reports say an applicant acts for an operator they do not name [RG-055]. Here the tenant has been named, but to investors, by a party to the lease, and not in the application the council must decide [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026] [SRC_WDDP_TPR].

The tenant is the company whose model drafted this essay [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026]. The question does not change for that.

### For journalists and researchers

Dexus's release was distributed as a regulatory announcement and can be read without the exchange's terms of use, through its distributor's copy [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026]. The planning report and its appendices sit on the council's application file under WDDP Pty Ltd [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. ## Our view

A council asked to approve what the ABC called the country's largest proposed data centre should not learn its tenant from a property group's announcement to investors.

Nothing here suggests anyone broke a rule. The application describes a land use, and a tenant may not be a planning matter. But the people of the Western Downs will live beside the campus, and it is their council that must weigh it. They have a stronger claim to the tenant's name than the share market does.

Where a lease, or an agreement to lease, exists for a proposed data centre, the applicant should tell the assessing authority who the tenant is, and the authority should publish it on the file. That applies to every tenant, including this one.

> To the Office of the Premier of Queensland: was the Western Downs Digital Park's tenant disclosed to any Queensland Government agency, or to Western Downs Regional Council, before Dexus announced it to the stock exchange? If so, when, and to whom?
