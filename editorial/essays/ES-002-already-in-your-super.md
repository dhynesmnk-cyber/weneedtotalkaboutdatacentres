## The common perception

In December 2024, AirTrunk announced that its sale to Blackstone and Canada's CPP Investments had closed at A$24 billion [SRC_AIRTRUNK_BC]. That is how data centre money usually reaches the news: overseas capital, building for overseas technology companies [SRC_AIRTRUNK_BC].

Some of it is ours. At 30 June 2026, the main options of three super funds held NEXTDC shares worth A$344.5 million, A$109.1 million and A$73.5 million [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606]. Their members own a slice of the build-out [RG-004].

## What the records show

### Three funds that let us in

Super funds publish what each investment option holds, twice a year [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606]. We set out to read the biggest funds' files, and three let us in [RG-004].

Cbus's Growth (MySuper) option held NEXTDC shares worth A$344,511,305.86, and NEXTDC notes worth A$18.6 million [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606]. It had also lent money to STACK Infrastructure Australia (Palmers Road) Pty Ltd, listed among its private debt [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606]. Hostplus's Balanced option held A$109,136,752 of NEXTDC, and HESTA's Balanced Growth option A$73,522,711 [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606].

Each figure is one option. The funds' other options hold their own amounts, which we have not added [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606] [RG-004].

### The fund that turned us away

AustralianSuper, UniSuper, Australian Retirement Trust, Aware Super and the Commonwealth's own super fund all turned our requests away [RG-004]. So did the Future Fund, after serving us one report [RG-094].

NEXTDC's annual report lists its substantial shareholders, from what they told the stock exchange up to 20 August 2026 [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT]. Second on the list, with 8.49 per cent of the shares, is AustralianSuper [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT]. The fund whose holdings we could not read appears in someone else's annual report [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT].

### Public money

The Future Fund invests for the Commonwealth. Its report at 31 December 2025, the one we hold, lists NEXTDC shares worth A$85.4 million across two lines, and CDC, another Australian data centre operator, at "29%" [SRC_FF_PIR_DEC25]. It also lists NVIDIA shares worth A$2,107,250,593, or 0.78 per cent of the fund [SRC_FF_PIR_DEC25].

CDC's own announcement of February 2025 gives the Future Fund a total stake of 34.55 per cent [SRC_CDC2025]. Neither document explains the gap [RG-094].

### The land under Mamre Road

The planned Mamre Road campus at Kemps Creek sits on a lot the title search registers to ISPT Pty Limited [SRC_SSD92743706_TITLE]. The national company register shows that company has been named IFM Real Estate Fiduciary since November 2025 [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. On the title sits a caveat lodged by KNBDC SYD4, now AirTrunk SYD4 [SRC_SSD92743706_TITLE] [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

All three funds that let us in have placed billions of dollars with IFM Investors [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606] [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606]. This record does not tell you whether any of that money is in the fund that owns the Mamre Road land. The trust behind the title, and the sale contract, would [RG-005].

### Overseas money too

The foreign money is real as well. By May 2026, NEXTDC's hybrid securities were backed by A$1.7 billion of binding commitments from La Caisse, "a global investment group based in Québec, Canada" [SRC_NXT_HYBRID_LA_CAISSE] [SRC_NXT_LIQUIDITY_MAY26]. The record holds both kinds of money, side by side [SRC_NXT_LIQUIDITY_MAY26] [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606].

## What to watch next

The funds publish these files twice a year, so the next set will show whether the holdings grew or shrank [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606]. The files we could not fetch are listed for a person to download, and the larger funds come first [RG-004].

### For journalists and researchers

Every fund's holdings file is public, and each lists its assets option by option. Search for the company's name, or for NEXTDC's share code "AU000000NXT8", which is how the Hostplus and HESTA files list it [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606].

## Our view

Super funds buy listed companies, and data centre builders are listed companies. Owning them is not a scandal.

But it changes who gets to ask questions. "Foreign money" makes an easy villain, and the record says some of the money is ours. A member whose savings sit in a data centre builder has standing to ask what that builder plans for their suburb's power and water, and what their fund asks on their behalf.

A fund can answer only if it counts. Shares, loans, and stakes in unlisted property and infrastructure all add up, and no fund file we read adds them up for this industry.

> To my super fund: what is the fund's total exposure to data centre owners and operators, by investment option, at the last disclosure date, counting shares, loans and unlisted property and infrastructure?
