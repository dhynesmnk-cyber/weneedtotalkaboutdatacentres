## The common perception

A NSW data centre application arrives with its own evidence: a noise assessment, an air quality assessment and, for the largest, a greenhouse gas report. When that evidence is checked, the letter calls the check "an independent review" [SRC_SSD9741_MOD3_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW].

The applicant engages the firm that writes the evidence, and the applicant or its consultant engages the firm that checks it [RG-075]. On three consents we read, the applicant was the consultancy that wrote the evidence [RG-073].

## What the records show

### Who writes the evidence

We opened the noise report and the air quality report for each of the 18 data centre consents we hold, and took the author from each report's own pages, not its filename [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS] [RG-073]. Ten firms wrote the 35 we could read [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS]. Two Arup companies wrote nine, SLR Consulting eight, Northstar Air Quality six and Pulse White Noise Acoustics four [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS].

Each is written for whoever commissioned it, and the cover says so: the Glendenning Road air quality report was "prepared for LCI Consultants by: Northstar Air Quality Pty Ltd" [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS]. The Department does not always keep the two apart. Its Station Road report lists the acoustic assessment as "prepared by LCI Consultants" [SRC_SSD33781208_ASSESSMENT]. The issue of that report we read names Pulse White Noise Acoustics as its author and LCI Consultants as its client [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS] [RG-073].

### When the applicant is the consultancy

Six of the consents are held in the name of a consultancy or design practice [RG-074]. In three, that firm also wrote the evidence [RG-073]:

- **43-61 Turner Road.** The consent names the applicant as "ARUP Pty Ltd", and its noise and air quality reports are Arup's [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS] [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS].
- **Project Echidna.** The applicant, ARUP Australia, "acts on behalf of a multinational data centre operation", the Department's report says, without naming it [SRC_SSD47320208_ASSESSMENT]. Its noise report is by Arup Australia [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS].
- **Davis Road.** The consent names "Cundall Johnston and Partners Pty Ltd" as applicant, and both reports carry Cundall's copyright [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS] [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS].

In each, the firm seeking consent wrote the evidence the Department weighed on noise and air [RG-073].

### The checks that exist

Acoustic peer review appears on the public files of two projects [SRC_ATTACHMENT_CONSULTANTS] [RG-075]. At DigiCo SYD1, Renzo Tonin & Associates "was engaged by Patch Planning, on behalf of HDI SYD1 Property Holdings Pty Limited (DigiCo)" to review Pulse White Noise Acoustics' assessment [SRC_SSD69637456_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW]. At Lane Cove West, the same firm was engaged by the applicant, EMKC Cubed Management, to review the same author's work [SRC_SSD9741_MOD3_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW].

Both letters say: "The peer review process did not involve any detailed modelling checks or remodelling by RTA." [SRC_SSD69637456_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW] [SRC_SSD9741_MOD3_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW] The reviewer read the drafts and commented. It did not run the numbers again.

Greenhouse gas is the one place a rule demands a check. For the largest emitters, the environment regulator's guide says "mitigation assessments must be verified by an independent expert review" [SRC_SSD10330_MOD4_GHG_REVIEW]. All three such reviews on the files are by one firm, Zephyr Environmental [SRC_SSD101067971_GHG_REVIEW] [SRC_SSD10330_MOD2_GHG_REVIEW] [SRC_SSD10330_MOD4_GHG_REVIEW].

At Project Atlas, "The Assessment Report has been prepared by LCI on behalf of Goodman Property Services", and the reviewer "has been engaged by LCI Consultants" [SRC_SSD101067971_GHG_REVIEW]. The author of the report hired its reviewer [RG-075].

Two of the letters state: "No actual or perceived conflicts of interest have come to my attention, and I have not been employed by the proponent within the last five years." [SRC_SSD101067971_GHG_REVIEW] [SRC_SSD10330_MOD2_GHG_REVIEW] The third, written for the same proponent's next modification at Roberts Road, carries no such statement [SRC_SSD10330_MOD4_GHG_REVIEW]. Each found the work "adequate" or "satisfactory" [SRC_SSD101067971_GHG_REVIEW] [SRC_SSD10330_MOD2_GHG_REVIEW].

### Who else looks

The environment regulator's own hold on a site's air emissions is a licence, and the reports say most of these sites need none [RG-008]. At 1-5 Khartoum Road, the Department's report records that "the EPA does not consider itself to be the appropriate regulatory authority for air emissions associated with the development" [SRC_SSD63235720_ASSESSMENT]. That leaves the Department, reading evidence the applicant bought [RG-075].

## Where the data is too thin

- Whether these firms also hold contracts with the Department that assesses their work. The contract registers refuse our crawler [RG-074].
- Who wrote the traffic, flooding, heritage and hazard reports [RG-073].
- Project Echidna's air quality report, too large for our crawler [RG-073].
- Whether the Department has ever commissioned a check of its own. None appears in the files we read [RG-075].

## What to watch next

Roberts Road's latest modification is "expected to involve the addition of 24 new diesel generators and 32 new chillers", and arrives with Zephyr's review attached [SRC_SSD10330_MOD4_GHG_REVIEW]. Whether the Department tests that evidence itself will show in its assessment report [RG-075].

### For journalists and researchers

A report's author is on its cover or document-control page, not in its filename [RG-073]. Read the "prepared for" line as well as the "prepared by": it tells you who commissioned the work [SRC_NSWPORTAL_TECH_REPORTS].

## Our view

Consultants are not the problem. Data centres need acoustic engineers and air quality modellers, and the firms in these files are the ones the industry uses.

The problem is the arrangement. The party that gains from approval buys the evidence, and when a check happens, it buys that too. A reviewer who reads drafts and does not rerun the model is checking the writing, not the result. Neighbours living beside generator yards are asked to trust both.

The greenhouse rule shows the State can require a second opinion. It should go further. The Department should commission noise and air quality reviews itself, recover the cost from the applicant, and name each report's author in its assessment.

> To the Secretary, NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure: will the Department name the author of every technical report in its assessment reports, and will it commission, rather than accept from the applicant, independent reviews of noise and air quality evidence for data centres?
