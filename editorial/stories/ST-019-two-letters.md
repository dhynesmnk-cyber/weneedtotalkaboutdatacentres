## What you heard
When a data centre breaks the conditions of its approval, does anyone find out [RG-058]?

## The hunt
Every approval comes with conditions, and with rules for reporting on them and for independent audits [SRC_SSD73761707_CONSENT].

So we searched the state's data centre files, close to seven thousand documents, for any sign that someone had been told off [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER] [RG-058]. Two turned up [RG-058].

On the file for CDC's Roberts Road data centre sits a document titled as the company's response to a warning letter [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER]. On the file for a data centre on Station Road, Seven Hills, sits a warning letter dated May 2024, with a department email about it [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].

We asked for each one. All of them are locked [RG-058]. So is a signed letter on the Roberts Road file [RG-058].

What the warnings were for, what the companies said, and whether anything followed, is not public [RG-058].

## What the record shows
- The Roberts Road record lists a "CDC Data Centres response warning letter" [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].
- The Station Road record lists a warning letter dated 29 May 2024 and an email about it [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].
- The Glendenning Road consent sets out compliance reporting and independent audits [SRC_SSD73761707_CONSENT].

## Still unknown
