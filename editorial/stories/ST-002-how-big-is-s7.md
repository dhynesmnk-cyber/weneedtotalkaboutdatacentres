## What you heard
NEXTDC's S7 at Eastern Creek in Sydney's west is billed as a 612 megawatt AI campus, to be built with OpenAI and valued at $7 billion [SRC_CERTSTRAT].

The NSW Government's planning website lists it at 612 megawatts too [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].

## The hunt
It sounds like the simplest question you could ask about a project worth more than $7 billion: how much power will it use [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]?

The company's own planning document gives two answers. In one place it says 612 megawatts; later in the same document, it says about 700 [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]. NEXTDC's website, meanwhile, said more than 550 [SRC_NEXTDC_S7].

None of those figures says how much would run the computers themselves, rather than the cooling and everything else on the site [RG-099]. For a while our own records guessed. Now they hold no figure at all, because a blank is honest and a guess is not [RG-099].

When we looked again in October, the project's public file had gained a new document, with "reject" in its title [RG-099]. It is locked, and we can't read it [RG-099].

Before anyone reads too much into that word: documents with the same label sit on the files of five other data centre projects, and one of them, Davis Road, went on to be approved [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER]. What this one says about S7 isn't public [RG-099].

## What the record shows
- The planning website lists S7 at 612 megawatts, with 340 back-up diesel generators [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].
- NEXTDC's planning document gives both 612 megawatts and about 700 as the site's total power [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING].
- The same document puts the investment at about $7.15 billion [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING].
- When we collected the records in September 2026, the project was at an early planning stage, its environmental impact statement not yet published [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].

## Still unknown
- How much of S7's power would go to the computers themselves [RG-099].
- What the locked document says. A freedom of information request could ask for it [RG-099].
- The deal between NEXTDC and OpenAI, which is a separate question (RG-014).

## Essay hooks
- A multi-billion-dollar project with three different answers to how big it is.
- Why our records would rather show a blank than a guess.
- A locked document with "reject" in its title, and what it does and doesn't mean.
