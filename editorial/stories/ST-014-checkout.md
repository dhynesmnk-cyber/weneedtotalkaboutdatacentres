## What you heard
After Coles said it was leaving Palantir, the campaign group GetUp told its supporters: "It’s only in the last few weeks, with the shareholder vote around the corner adding yet another layer of pressure, that Coles changed tack." [SRC_ACS_COLES_SEP26]

## The hunt
In early 2024 Coles brought in Palantir's software to plan rosters, stores and supply across more than 840 supermarkets [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26]. A campaign followed: a petition with 86,000 signatures and a push for a shareholder vote [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26].

In September 2026 Coles said it was over [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26]. "Coles and Palantir have agreed to conclude their partnership after three years of collaboration," a spokesperson said [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26] [SRC_CYBERDAILY_COLES_SEP26]. It is not expected to run past 2027 [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26].

That was the whole explanation. The rest of the statement praised the software, said it runs inside Coles' own systems, and denied that Palantir had any access to its security cameras [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26] [SRC_ACS_COLES_SEP26]. "Palantir does not control the data, nor can it independently repurpose it" [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26].

We looked for Coles saying more in its own documents, and found no release [RG-095]. Its notice for this year's annual meeting, sent in September, lists five items of business, and none is about Palantir: the vote GetUp wanted is not there [SRC_COLES_AGM_NOM_2026].

GetUp's statement went further, saying Palantir "still has lucrative contracts with Westpac" [SRC_CYBERDAILY_COLES_SEP26]. We found no record of one [RG-088]. Palantir's own announcements do name other Australian customers: the equipment dealer WesTrac in Perth, since 2021, and the miner Rio Tinto [SRC_PALANTIR_WESTRAC_2022].

## What the record shows
- On 4 September 2026 a Coles spokesperson said Coles and Palantir "have agreed to conclude their partnership after three years of collaboration", with the arrangement not expected beyond 2027 [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26].
- Coles also said Palantir had no access to its CCTV, facial recognition or crime-prevention measures [SRC_ACS_COLES_SEP26].
- Coles' 2026 notice of meeting has no item about Palantir [SRC_COLES_AGM_NOM_2026].
- Palantir announced WesTrac as a customer in November 2022 [SRC_PALANTIR_WESTRAC_2022].

## Still unknown
- Whether the campaign changed Coles' mind. Coles has not said, and only Coles can [RG-095].
- Whether Westpac uses Palantir. One report rests on a social media post; nothing else has been found [RG-088].

## Essay hooks
- Who gets the credit when a company changes its mind and gives no reason.
- A customer's denial as the only public account of what the software did.
- Loud pressure on a private contract; silence on the public ones.
