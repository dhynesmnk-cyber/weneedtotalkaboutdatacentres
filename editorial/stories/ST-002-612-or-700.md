## What you heard
NSW's planning portal describes NEXTDC's S7 at Eastern Creek as "a data centre with an operational capacity of 612 MW" [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].

A directory site calls it "a 612MW hyperscale AI campus and GPU supercluster", to be developed with OpenAI and "valued at AUD $7 billion" [SRC_CERTSTRAT].

## The hunt
A megawatt figure on a data centre can mean two different things: the power that runs the computers, called the IT load, or everything the site draws, cooling included [RG-099]. The portal's description does not say which, so our records first stored S7's 612 MW as IT load [RG-099].

The portal lists S7 at the "Prepare EIS" stage: its environmental impact statement was still being prepared when the records were collected [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER]. The fullest public document is the scoping report NEXTDC filed to ask what the assessment must cover, and we saved a copy on 10 October [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING].

It does not settle the number. Page 27 lists "Power Consumption 612MW" [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]. Page 40 says the data centre "will have a total power consumption of 612 megawatts" [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]. But page 35 says "The expected total site load is approximately 700MW, with infrastructure designed to support up to 875MVA" [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING]. NEXTDC's own S7 page, read in September, put the facility at more than 550 MW [SRC_NEXTDC_S7].

None of these calls 612 MW the IT load, and the two totals disagree [RG-099]. Rather than pick one, we took S7's figure out of every capacity column, so our records now hold no capacity for it at all [RG-099].

While re-reading the live portal record on 14 October, we found a new attachment titled "issued-reject-eis" [RG-099]. It answered 401, meaning it is not public, so we have not read it [RG-099]. The same kind of document appears on the records of five other data centre projects, including Davis Road, which was approved in December 2024 [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER]. Its title alone does not tell us what happened to S7 [RG-099].

## What the record shows
- The portal describes S7 as "a data centre with an operational capacity of 612 MW" with "340 back-up diesel generators" [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].
- Its scoping report calls 612 MW its "total power consumption" and also expects a "total site load" of "approximately 700MW" [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING].
- The same report gives an expected capital investment value of $7,154,563,995 [SRC_SSD113934740_SCOPING].
- S7 was at the "Prepare EIS" stage when the portal's records were collected in September 2026 [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].

## Still unknown
- How much of S7's power would run the computers themselves. No public document gives an IT load [RG-099].
- What the restricted attachment says. A request under NSW's freedom of information law could ask for it [RG-099].
- The commercial terms between NEXTDC and OpenAI, which are a separate question (RG-014).

## Essay hooks
- A single megawatt figure can describe the computers or the whole site, and a headline rarely says which.
- What a neighbour can learn about a project before its environmental impact statement is public.
- Why a document titled as a rejection can sit on the record of a project that went on to be approved.
