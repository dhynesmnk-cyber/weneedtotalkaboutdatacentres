## What you heard
"Data centre operators do not receive subsidies or tax incentives," the industry's peak body told the NSW inquiry [SRC_INQ_T_0501].

## The hunt
We went looking for the bill, and found it written into the approvals themselves [RG-025]. Most of the NSW consents we hold say what the developer must pay before building starts [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS].

The amounts are all over the place. A data centre in Camden owes its council about $8.6 million [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS]. An expansion in the City of Sydney owes the State $3,568.10 [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS]. Several approvals carry no payment condition at all [RG-025].

Microsoft's Kemps Creek campus is to pay through a separate deal with Penrith Council: $8.4 million in the draft, for parks and public works [SRC_PENRITH_VPA_NOTE] [SRC_PENRITH_VPA_DRAFT]. The council publishes the signed version too, but only as a scan, so its figure could not be read [SRC_PENRITH_VPA] [RG-025].

And tax? Nobody from the tax office appeared at the inquiry [RG-007]. The head of the Premier's Department said: "The one thing that we don't contemplate is government participation financially." [SRC_INQ_T_0522]

What the State gives instead is help. The 2025-26 Budget put $17.7 million into an authority that speeds up big projects [SRC_NSW_IDA_BUDGET_2025]. The day it was announced, the government's own media release carried quotes from NEXTDC and AirTrunk welcoming it [SRC_NSW_IDA_BUDGET_2025]. Its first round endorsed data centre projects worth billions [SRC_NSW_IDA].

The planning department is also creating a "concierge" for data centre developers [SRC_NSWGUIDE26]. How many people staff it, and what it costs, is not published [RG-063].

## What the record shows
- The NSW data centre consents we hold set payments to the council and the State from $3,568.10 to about $8.6 million [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS].
- Penrith's draft agreement with Microsoft sets $8,423,505.96, indexed, before construction [SRC_PENRITH_VPA_NOTE] [SRC_PENRITH_VPA_DRAFT].
- The 2025-26 NSW Budget gave the Investment Delivery Authority $17.7 million [SRC_NSW_IDA_BUDGET_2025].
- The State's Data Centre Guidelines commit to a concierge function for data centre proponents [SRC_NSWGUIDE26].

## Still unknown
- Whether the signed Penrith agreement kept the draft's figure, and whether any of these amounts has been paid [RG-025].
- Whether any tax concession exists. An application to the tax office is drafted, asking for its rules and totals, not any one company [RG-007].
- The concierge's staff and cost, and whether councils or residents can get the same service [RG-063].

## Essay hooks
- "No subsidies" can be true and still miss what the State gives in time and attention.
- The same industry, very different bills, depending on the council and the year.
- A government release that reads, in places, like the industry's.
