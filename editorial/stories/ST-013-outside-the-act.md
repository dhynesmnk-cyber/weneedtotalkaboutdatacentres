## What you heard
When Palantir announced in November 2025 that it had passed a government security assessment, it said its Australian platform is "hosted in Australian Amazon Web Services (AWS) regions" [SRC_PALANTIR_IRAP_NOV25].

## The hunt
Australian data, kept in Australia: that is the promise [SRC_PALANTIR_IRAP_NOV25]. The government keeps its own list of the cloud services certified to hold its data [SRC_HCF_REGISTER]. Amazon's Australian regions are on it. Palantir is not [SRC_HCF_REGISTER].

That might mean nothing. Palantir's promise is about where its platform runs, and Amazon is where it says it runs [SRC_PALANTIR_IRAP_NOV25] [SRC_HCF_REGISTER]. But does each government contract use that platform? None of the 29 contracts says where anything is hosted [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610]. Eleven were signed with the American parent company, not its Australian arm [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].

So we went to ask, and found the door already shut. Australia's freedom of information law lists the agencies it does not reach, and the Australian Signals Directorate, the signals intelligence agency, is one [SRC_FOI_ACT_1982]. Any document that came from it is out of reach too, whichever agency holds it [SRC_FOI_ACT_1982]. Five of its six Palantir contracts are with the American parent [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].

The same wall stands in front of a puzzle. A news investigation reported that the agency gave Palantir "a top security clearance" in November 2024 [SRC_CRIKEY_DEF_FEB26]. Palantir's own announcement of its security assessment came a year later [SRC_PALANTIR_IRAP_NOV25]. Two dates, a year apart, for what may be two different things, and the agency at the centre cannot be asked [SRC_FOI_ACT_1982] [RG-089].

Defence can be asked. Two applications to it are drafted: one on where its Palantir systems run, one on what clearances Defence itself recorded [RG-087] [RG-089].

## What the record shows
- Palantir says its Australian platform is hosted in Amazon Web Services' Australian regions [SRC_PALANTIR_IRAP_NOV25].
- The government's register of certified cloud services lists Amazon's Australian regions and does not list Palantir [SRC_HCF_REGISTER].
- The Freedom of Information Act lists the Australian Signals Directorate as an exempt agency [SRC_FOI_ACT_1982].
- None of the Commonwealth's 29 Palantir contracts records where the software is hosted [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].

## Still unknown
- Where each Defence deployment runs, and whether any data leaves Australia. An application to Defence is drafted [RG-087].
- What, if anything, was granted in November 2024, and by whom [RG-089].
