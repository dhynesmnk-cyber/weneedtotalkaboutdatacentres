## What you heard
A data centre campus proposed for Queensland's Western Downs is designed for "approximately 2,160MW of IT load", and its planning report names one company behind it: WDDP Pty Ltd [SRC_WDDP_TPR].

## The hunt
The planning report is dated 14 August 2026 [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. The national company register shows WDDP Pty Ltd was created on 28 July 2026, seventeen days earlier [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

Trade reports said the developer was a company called Zerra, owned by an investment firm called AGP [SRC_DCD_ZERRA]. The application never mentions either [RG-050].

The register does. A cluster of Zerra companies shares the project's initials, and three of them were created on 10 August 2026, four days before the report was dated [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. Another, Zerra WDDP Manager Australia, took its current name on 13 August, the day before [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. Among its former names: AGP DC Manager Australia [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

The application went to Western Downs Regional Council a few days later [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. The company asking a regional council to approve a campus of this size had existed for less than three weeks [SRC_WDDP_TPR] [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

## What the record shows
- The planning report, dated 14 August 2026, was prepared for WDDP Pty Ltd and describes approximately 2,160 megawatts of IT load [SRC_WDDP_TPR].
- WDDP Pty Ltd was registered on 28 July 2026 [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].
- Three Zerra WDDP companies were registered on 10 August 2026, and Zerra WDDP Manager Australia took its current name on 13 August 2026 [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].
- The former names of two Zerra companies are AGP DC Manager Australia and AGPDC Investments Australia [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].
