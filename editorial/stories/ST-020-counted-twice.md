## What you heard
How many data centres has Stockland been approved to build in Macquarie Park, and who are they for?

## The hunt
For two months our own records held two Stockland data centres in the same Macquarie Park business park [RG-084]. One came from the state's signed approval [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS]. The other came from a directory website, and it said much more: five storeys, about $264 million, reportedly for Amazon Web Services, with building already under way [RG-084].

Then we read the Department's assessment of the approved project. It describes a building that "presents as a five-storey warehouse building", at a cost of about $263.6 million [SRC_SSD10467_ASSESSMENT]. Same street, same company group, same size, same price. It was one project, written down twice [RG-084].

So where had Amazon come from? We went back to the directory page we had cited. The word Stockland does not appear in it [SRC_CERTSTRAT]. Neither the approval nor the assessment names a tenant or a start date [RG-102]. The second record went, and the Amazon claim went with it, kept only as a question [RG-084].

Next door, it nearly happened again. Two more Stockland applications, one approved and one still being assessed, give the same street address and the same parcel of land [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS] [SRC_SSD80814238_EIS]. One is for up to 76.4 megawatts, the other for 34.3 megawatts of computing load [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS] [SRC_SSD80814238_EIS]. This time we have not merged them, and we have not added them together: until a plan shows where each building sits, a total could count the same ground twice [RG-105].

## What the record shows
- The approved Macquarie Park project "presents as a five-storey warehouse building", at a cost of about $263.6 million [SRC_SSD10467_ASSESSMENT].
- Its approval names Stockland Trust Management Limited as the applicant [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS].
- The directory page once cited for an Amazon tenant does not mention Stockland at all [SRC_CERTSTRAT].
- The approved project at 1-5 Khartoum Road and the Road 1 application describe the same parcel of land [SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS] [SRC_SSD80814238_EIS].
- The Road 1 application says "The land is owned by Stockland" [SRC_SSD80814238_EIS].

## Still unknown
- Who the approved Macquarie Park building is for, and whether work has started [RG-102]. Stockland's own announcements, or a construction certificate on the public file, would say.
- The state's list of endorsed data centre projects says one is left off "due to commercial sensitivities" [SRC_NSW_IDA]. Whether that is this one is not known [RG-102].
- Whether Road 1 and its approved neighbour are two buildings on one campus or one project recorded twice [RG-105]. A site plan showing both footprints would settle it.

## Essay hooks
- How one building became two in a public record, and how close it came to happening again next door.
- An Amazon tenant a website reported, which no document we could find supports.
- Why a careful record refuses to add up figures it cannot place on a map.
