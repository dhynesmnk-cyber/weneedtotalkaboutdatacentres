## What you heard
In March 2026 the NSW Government endorsed a data centre campus at Mamre Road in Kemps Creek, and named the company behind it: "KNBDC SYD4 Pty Ltd" [SRC_NSW_IDA].

The planning website lists the campus at "a power capacity of 1 GW" [SRC_NSWPORTAL_REGISTER].

## The hunt
KNBDC. Four letters, shared by a whole stack of trusts and finance companies [RG-021].

The environmental impact statement names a different company again as the proponent: Plan Project Management Pty Ltd [SRC_SSD92743706_EIS]. And the trade press had a third name, for the likely buyer of the land: AirTrunk, if the campus was approved [SRC_WMEDIA_ISPT].

So we searched the national company register, which the government publishes free each month. KNBDC SYD4 Pty Ltd was created in June 2025 [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. On 16 June 2026 it took a new name: AirTrunk SYD4 Pty Ltd [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. Its sister companies changed the same week, and so did several KNBDC companies in Melbourne [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

The government had endorsed the project under a name that, less than three months later, became AirTrunk's [SRC_NSW_IDA] [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

The land title filed with the application fills in the rest. The registered owner is ISPT, a company the register now names IFM Real Estate Fiduciary [SRC_SSD92743706_TITLE] [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. On the title sits a caveat, a formal claim on someone else's land, lodged by KNBDC SYD4 [SRC_SSD92743706_TITLE].

What the free register cannot say is who owns AirTrunk SYD4 Pty Ltd. That takes a paid search, and it is first on our list [RG-036].

## What the record shows
- The NSW Government's March 2026 list names KNBDC SYD4 Pty Ltd as the company behind the Mamre Road Data Centre Campus [SRC_NSW_IDA].
- KNBDC SYD4 Pty Ltd was registered in June 2025 and has been named AirTrunk SYD4 Pty Ltd since 16 June 2026 [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].
- The title filed with the application shows the land registered to ISPT, with a caveat lodged by KNBDC SYD4 [SRC_SSD92743706_TITLE].
- ISPT Pty Ltd has been registered as IFM Real Estate Fiduciary since November 2025 [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610].

## Still unknown
- Who owns AirTrunk SYD4 Pty Ltd [RG-036].
- What interest the caveat protects, and the price and conditions of any sale [RG-005].
- What the landowners' consent letters from IFM, GPT and Gibb Group say. All three are locked [RG-021].
