## What you heard
On 5 December 2025, NEXTDC told the stock exchange it would "Join OpenAI in Australia as an Infrastructure Partner", starting with a large AI campus called S7 in Sydney's west [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025] [SRC_NEXTDC_S7].

By July 2026, Reuters was calling S7 "OpenAI's Australian data centre" [SRC_REUTERS_S7].

## The hunt
What had OpenAI actually signed? NEXTDC's own announcement answers it: a memorandum of understanding, to "collaborate on the planning, development and operation" of the campus [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025]. It names no price, no length of contract, no amount of space and no stake [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025].

A week later, NEXTDC lodged its response to a compliance letter from the exchange [SRC_ASX_NXT_ANNOUNCEMENTS_2025]. The reply sits behind the exchange's terms of use, so a person must read it before we can say what was asked [RG-014].

Then we read the half-year and full-year results NEXTDC has posted since, its annual report and three of its fundraising announcements. OpenAI is not mentioned once [RG-014].

S7 kept moving. In February, NEXTDC said the NSW Government had put S7 on its fast track [SRC_NXT_1H26_PRESENTATION]. The Government's own list, released in March, names it [SRC_NSW_IDA].

By August, the size of S7's first stage read "TBD", and NEXTDC was looking at bringing in outside investors through a joint venture [SRC_NXT_FY26_PRESENTATION]. Its annual report lists companies named for S7, all still wholly owned by NEXTDC [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT]. NEXTDC's own S7 web page does not mention OpenAI [SRC_NEXTDC_S7].

We tried OpenAI's website too. It turned our requests away [RG-014].

## What the record shows
- NEXTDC's 5 December 2025 announcement describes a memorandum of understanding with OpenAI, with the first stage of S7 expected in the second half of 2027, subject to approvals [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025].
- The announcement states no price, contract length, capacity commitment or ownership stake [SRC_NXT_OPENAI_MOU_2025].
- NEXTDC lodged a response to an exchange compliance letter on 12 December 2025 [SRC_ASX_NXT_ANNOUNCEMENTS_2025].
- NEXTDC's FY26 results presentation lists the initial size of S7 as "TBD" and says NEXTDC is weighing outside capital through a joint venture [SRC_NXT_FY26_PRESENTATION].

## Still unknown
- What OpenAI has committed to S7, if anything beyond the memorandum [RG-014].
- What the exchange asked NEXTDC, and what NEXTDC answered [RG-014].
- Whether the database should still record OpenAI as the main tenant of S7. That is a decision for the author [RG-014].

## Essay hooks
- A headline partnership that leaves no trace in the company's own accounts.
- Who gets to call a building "OpenAI's" when the public record holds only a memorandum.
- What a government fast track means when the partner's commitment is not public.
