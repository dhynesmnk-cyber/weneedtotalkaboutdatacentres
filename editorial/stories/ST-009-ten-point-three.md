## What you heard
On 1 May 2026, the data centre industry's peak body opened its evidence to a NSW parliamentary inquiry with this: "Data centre operators will pay $10.3 billion in energy infrastructure to 2030, and $1.1 billion of that is excess capacity available for public use." [SRC_INQ_T_0501]

## The hunt
The number had to come from somewhere, and it did: the industry's own report, published the November before [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025]. Four operators commissioned it: AirTrunk, Amazon Web Services, CDC and NEXTDC [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025].

The report never says data centres will pay $10.3 billion [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025]. It says they spent $3.1 billion on the grid between 2020 and 2025, and will spend $7.2 billion more by 2030 [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025]. Add the two together and you get the figure the inquiry heard [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025] [SRC_INQ_T_0501].

The report does use the words "$10.3 billion" once [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025]. They describe what the Australian Government has committed to the grid [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025].

The $1.1 billion "for public use" is in the report too, but as part of the forecast, not money already spent [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025]. And the report's figures came from "select data centre operators": the industry counting its own spending [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025].

So we went to the people who own the wires. The electricity networks told the inquiry that in NSW "the customer who caused network costs pays for them" [SRC_INQ_T_0529]. Transgrid said it builds nothing until a customer has fully committed, and that no data centre had yet [SRC_INQ_T_0522]. Not one of them put a total on what data centres pay [RG-043].

## What the record shows
- Data Centres Australia told the inquiry that operators will pay $10.3 billion in energy infrastructure to 2030 [SRC_INQ_T_0501].
- The report behind the industry's figures, commissioned by AirTrunk, AWS, CDC and NEXTDC, gives $3.1 billion spent from 2020 to 2025 and $7.2 billion forecast to 2030 [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025].
- The only "$10.3 billion" the report itself states is the Australian Government's grid funding [SRC_MANDALA_DCEI_2025].
- On 22 May 2026, Transgrid said no data centre had yet committed to a connection on its network [SRC_INQ_T_0522].

## Still unknown
- Whether the operators' own figures match what the networks have recorded and the regulator has approved [RG-043].
- How much of the forecast spending is signed, and how much is hope [RG-043].

## Essay hooks
- A reassuring number, traced back to the people it reassures about.
- The principle everyone agrees on, and the total nobody can check.
- Who should count what an industry pays: the industry, or the people it pays?
