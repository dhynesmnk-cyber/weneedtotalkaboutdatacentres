## What you heard
The NSW Government's data centre rules, published in August 2026, tell the public that "Oxford Economics, which prepared AEMO's forecast on data centres, estimates 6 in every 7 megawatts (MW) of data centre connection requests are phantom demand." [SRC_NSWGUIDE26]

AEMO is the Australian Energy Market Operator, which runs the national electricity market [SRC_NSWGUIDE26].

## The hunt
Six in seven: most of the power data centres ask for is not expected to be needed [SRC_OXEC_PHANTOM_2025]. It became the number everyone agreed on. Data Centres Australia and the Business Council of Australia both cited it to the inquiry [SRC_INQ_T_0522] [SRC_BCA_SUB116]. Then the government's own rules did [SRC_NSWGUIDE26].

The rules describe the firm by its work for the market operator, and that work is real [SRC_NSWGUIDE26] [SRC_OXEC_PHANTOM_2025]. But the paper the rules cite is a different job. Its last line reads: "This research briefing was commissioned by AWS." [SRC_OXEC_PHANTOM_2025] That is Amazon's cloud business. The rules never say who commissioned it [SRC_NSWGUIDE26].

Who else is in the room? The Business Council publishes its member list [SRC_BCA_MEMBERS_202610]. On it are AirTrunk and its owner Blackstone, Amazon, Google, Microsoft and Meta, the developers Goodman and Stockland, and two of the networks that connect data centres, TransGrid and Endeavour Energy [SRC_BCA_MEMBERS_202610] [SRC_AIRTRUNK_BC] [SRC_INQ_T_0522] [SRC_INQ_T_0529].

We tried to see the meetings. Ministers publish their diaries every three months, but the government's website tells automated readers to keep out of the folder they are kept in [SRC_NSW_DIARIES_2026] [RG-070]. The state's lobbyist register turns them away entirely [RG-070].

What we have is the inquiry. The head of the Premier's Department said he had met Data Centres Australia about the government's draft policy [SRC_INQ_T_0522]. The head of Infrastructure NSW said "a lot of those organisations have made direct representations to different people in government" [SRC_INQ_T_0522].

## What the record shows
- Oxford Economics' briefing on phantom demand says it "was commissioned by AWS" [SRC_OXEC_PHANTOM_2025].
- The NSW Data Centre Guidelines cite that briefing and introduce the firm as the one that prepared the market operator's forecast [SRC_NSWGUIDE26].
- The Business Council's members include AirTrunk, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Blackstone, Goodman, Stockland, TransGrid and Endeavour Energy [SRC_BCA_MEMBERS_202610].
- The head of the Premier's Department told the inquiry he met Data Centres Australia about the consultation paper [SRC_INQ_T_0522].
