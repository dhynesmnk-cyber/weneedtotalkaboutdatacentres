## What you heard
"Australia's largest proposed data centre could draw a quarter of the state's peak power," the ABC reported in September 2026 [SRC_ABC_WD].

## The hunt
The question was simple: who is it for? The developer's application to the Western Downs council runs to hundreds of pages [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. It describes the farmland near Dalby, the buildings, the power and a camp for construction workers [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. It never names the customer [SRC_WDDP_TPR]. It does not name the company behind the project either, only a company called WDDP Pty Ltd [SRC_WDDP_TPR].

Reports said the customer was Anthropic, the American AI company [RG-012]. Reports were not enough: we needed a party to the deal, on the record [RG-012].

In September, one spoke. Dexus, the property group, told the stock exchange that a consortium it is part of, with Zerra DC and Macquarie Capital, "has entered into lease documentation with the Australian subsidiary of Anthropic for the delivery of the first stage of the campus, subject to customary approvals" [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026].

So investors heard the tenant's name from a party to the deal, while the application the council is weighing still does not name it [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026] [SRC_WDDP_TPR].

One more thing the reader should know. Anthropic makes the AI model that drafted this card, so every line here points to a document the author can read for themselves [RG-012].

## What the record shows
- The application lodged with Western Downs Regional Council in August 2026 was prepared for WDDP Pty Ltd and does not mention Anthropic or Zerra [SRC_WDDP_TPR].
- On 16 September 2026 Dexus told the stock exchange that a consortium of Zerra DC, Macquarie Capital and its Australian Data Centres business had entered into lease documentation with Anthropic's Australian subsidiary, subject to customary approvals [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026].
- Dexus's data centre business holds 25% of that consortium [SRC_DEXUS_WDDP_2026].
