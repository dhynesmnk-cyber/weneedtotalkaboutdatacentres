## What you heard
In December 2024 the sale of AirTrunk to Blackstone and CPP Investments closed at A$24 billion, the largest data centre deal on record [SRC_AIRTRUNK_BC].

So is this overseas money building for overseas giants, or is some of it ours?

## The hunt
Super funds publish what each of their investment options owns, twice a year [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606]. Cbus says it is required to [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606].

We set out to read them. AustralianSuper, UniSuper, Australian Retirement Trust, Aware Super, the Commonwealth's own super fund and the Future Fund all turned our requests away [RG-004] [RG-094].

Three funds let us in. At the end of June, Cbus's Growth option held A$344.5 million of shares in NEXTDC, the listed data centre builder [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606]. It had lent NEXTDC money too, and a company called STACK Infrastructure Australia (Palmers Road) sits on its list of borrowers [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606]. Hostplus's Balanced option held A$109.1 million of NEXTDC, and HESTA's Balanced Growth option A$73.5 million [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606].

Then the twist. NEXTDC's annual report lists its substantial shareholders, from what they told the stock exchange. Second on the list, with 8.49% of the shares, is AustralianSuper, the fund that had turned us away [SRC_NXT_FY26_ANNUAL_REPORT].

Public money is in as well. The Future Fund's last report we hold puts its stake in CDC at 29% [SRC_FF_PIR_DEC25]. CDC's own announcement says 34.55% [SRC_CDC2025]. Neither explains the gap [RG-094].

The land under the Mamre Road campus is registered to a company now called IFM Real Estate Fiduciary [SRC_SSD92743706_TITLE] [SRC_ASIC_COMPANIES_202610]. All three funds that let us in had put billions with IFM [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606] [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606].

Overseas money is in the mix too. La Caisse, an investment group from Québec, has made A$1.7 billion of binding commitments to NEXTDC's fundraising [SRC_NXT_HYBRID_LA_CAISSE] [SRC_NXT_LIQUIDITY_MAY26].

## What the record shows
- At 30 June 2026, Cbus's Growth option held A$344.5 million of NEXTDC shares, Hostplus's Balanced option A$109.1 million and HESTA's Balanced Growth option A$73.5 million [SRC_CBUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HOSTPLUS_PHD_202606] [SRC_HESTA_PHD_202606].
