## What you heard
An online video put the fear in its title: NVIDIA and Palantir, "Global Surveillance, 'Pre-Crime' Arrests, & AI" [SRC_GNCA_VIDEO].

## The hunt
In mid-2026 a police van in Perth began scanning faces in crowds, looking for wanted people [SRC_GUARDIAN_WA_FRT]. In its first week it scanned more than 130,000 faces and led to 19 arrests, one of them live at the launch press conference [SRC_GUARDIAN_WA_FRT]. The news of the launch did not say whose software it was [SRC_GUARDIAN_WA_FRT].

But the police had written it down. WA Police's privacy assessment for the trial names the supplier: NEC, whose NeoFace Watch system London's Metropolitan Police uses [SRC_WAPOL_OLFR_PIA]. It promises that the data is stored in Australia [SRC_WAPOL_OLFR_PIA]. Palantir is not mentioned [SRC_WAPOL_OLFR_PIA].

So the scariest picture belongs to someone else. Palantir's place in Australia's crime-fighting agencies is quieter, and older [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610]. The Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission has bought its software since 2014: five contracts, about $6.1 million [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610]. AUSTRAC, which tracks suspicious money, has bought it since 2016: six contracts, about $28.1 million [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610]. Not one contract says what the software does [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].

One trade report states, without a source, that Defence uses Palantir's system to help choose military targets [SRC_ITNEWS_COLES_SEP26]. No contract or Defence record we have read says so [RG-093].

## What the record shows
- WA Police's privacy assessment names NEC as the vendor of its live facial recognition trial and states that data is stored in Australia [SRC_WAPOL_OLFR_PIA].
- The ACIC has published five Palantir contracts since 2014, worth $6,068,875.14 [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].
- AUSTRAC has published six since 2016, worth $28,117,548.41 [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].
- No Commonwealth contract with Palantir names the Australian Federal Police or Home Affairs as the buyer [SRC_AUSTENDER_OCDS_202610].

## Still unknown
