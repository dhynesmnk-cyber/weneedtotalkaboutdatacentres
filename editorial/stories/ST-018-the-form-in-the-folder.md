## What you heard
The NSW planning website tells the public that the Independent Planning Commission is generally the consent authority when "the applicant has made a reportable political donation" [SRC_NSWPORTAL_DETERMINE_DA].

## The hunt
Anyone applying to build a major project must declare their political donations on a form filed with the application [SRC_SSD10330_DONATION]. Across every data centre file in NSW, we found three such forms [RG-069]. One is locked [RG-069].

The second was typed and plain. A company connected to Macquarie Data Centres gave two Liberal Party branches $2,000 and $1,500, and a council campaign group in Ryde, where the project sits, $1,980 [SRC_SSD24299707_DONATION]. That, with the council's objection, sent the Talavera Road expansion to the commission [SRC_DPE_TALAVERA_REFERRAL] [SRC_IPC_TALAVERA_SOR]. The commission's own statement names the two commissioners who decided; the copy of the consent on the planning website leaves their names blank [SRC_IPC_TALAVERA_SOR] [RG-085].

The third was a scan, filed on its side, with no text a computer could read [SRC_SSD10330_DONATION]. We turned it up the right way and read it. In December 2019, CDC's director and chief executive, filing as the applicant for its Roberts Road data centre, declared a $7,500 donation to the Liberal Party's NSW division, made on 18 June 2018 [SRC_SSD10330_DONATION]. It was declared, as the law requires [SRC_SSD10330_DONATION].

Then we read the department's decision. An officer could decide the case only where "a political disclosure statement has not been made" [SRC_SSD10330_ASSESSMENT]. The report said: "No reportable political donations were made by the Applicant in the last two years" [SRC_SSD10330_ASSESSMENT]. An executive director in the department approved Roberts Road in July 2020 [SRC_SSD10330_ASSESSMENT].

Nothing in the record explains why the form made no difference [RG-069]. An application to the department now asks [RG-069].

## What the record shows
- The Roberts Road disclosure, signed on 2 December 2019, declares a $7,500 donation to the Liberal Party of Australia NSW Division on 18 June 2018 [SRC_SSD10330_DONATION].
- The department's report says its delegation applied where a political disclosure statement had not been made, and that the applicant made no reportable donations in the previous two years [SRC_SSD10330_ASSESSMENT].
- The Talavera Road disclosure lists three donations by Macquarie Telecom, totalling $5,480 [SRC_SSD24299707_DONATION].
- The commission decided Talavera Road because of Ryde's objection and the connected company's donations [SRC_IPC_TALAVERA_SOR].

## Still unknown
- Why the Roberts Road disclosure did not send the case to the commission. An application under the state's right-to-information law is drafted [RG-069].
- What the third form, filed on the Mamre Road campus, declares. It is locked [RG-069].
