## What you heard
The Australian Government says its top certification for data centres "represents the highest level of assurance to Australian Government customers and offers the most secure storage solutions for government held data" [SRC_HCF_REGISTER].

## The hunt
The government's list of its most trusted data centres is public [SRC_HCF_REGISTER]. When we read it end to end, we found thirteen certified facilities our own records had never heard of [RG-101]. The list gives a company and a name, and nothing else: no state, no street, no sign of whether the place is even running [RG-101].

Eight we could place from the operators' own websites: two in Canberra, three in south-east Queensland, two Fujitsu sites in Sydney and one in Macquarie Park [RG-101]. One of those pages brought a surprise. Macquarie says one of its buildings there is still under construction, though our records had it running [SRC_OP_MACQUARIE_IC3_SUPER_WEST] [RG-104].

The last five were harder. Two operators' websites carry a sign telling automated readers to keep out, and the stock exchange showed us a robot check instead of a company's own announcements [RG-101]. We did not go around them.

Then a person downloaded three of DigiCo's documents by hand [RG-101]. Its annual report lists Brisbane 2 and Adelaide 1 among its seven Australian properties [SRC_DGT_AR_2026]. And its Sydney site, already in our records as a planning approval, turned out to be a working data centre being expanded from the inside, holding the government's top certification [SRC_DGT_SYD1_71MW_20261015] [SRC_DGT_AR_2026].

In October, DigiCo announced a 15-year contract for 71 megawatts there with "a global technology firm" [SRC_DGT_SYD1_71MW_20261015]. It did not say which one [SRC_DGT_SYD1_71MW_20261015].

## What the record shows
- The government describes its top certification as "the highest level of assurance to Australian Government customers" [SRC_HCF_REGISTER].
- DigiCo's annual report lists seven Australian properties, among them Brisbane 2 and Adelaide 1 [SRC_DGT_AR_2026].
- DigiCo says its Sydney site holds "Strategic Certification" under the government's framework [SRC_DGT_AR_2026].
- DigiCo calls its Sydney expansion "the adaptive reuse of an established data centre asset" [SRC_DGT_SYD1_71MW_20261015].
- It has contracted 71 megawatts there to a global technology firm it does not name [SRC_DGT_SYD1_71MW_20261015].
- Macquarie's own page says its IC3 Super West building is under construction [SRC_OP_MACQUARIE_IC3_SUPER_WEST].

## Still unknown
- Which technology firm has the Sydney contract [RG-101].
- Where Brisbane 2 and Adelaide 1 are, and how big they are [RG-103].
- Where two more certified facilities are, Cloud Carrier's DB1 and Vocus's TransACT House. Asking the operators directly is the route left [RG-101].
- Where DigiCo's other four Australian properties are [RG-106].
- Whether the "IC3" on the government's list is the Macquarie building still under construction [RG-104].
