# FOI-004: what the ACIC runs on Palantir, from the Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission

Status: **drafted**, 2026-10-22. Not lodged.

To: the Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission, under the Freedom of
Information Act 1982 (Cth). Lodge through the FOI page on acic.gov.au, and
check the lodgement address there before sending.

Cost: no application fee; processing charges may apply (ask for a waiver
below). Decision due within 30 days, plus 30 if third parties are consulted.

## Why this request

The ACIC has published five contracts with Palantir since 2014, worth
$6,068,875.14 at their latest values: software licences, maintenance and
support (RG-086). None says what the software is for. One, CN3996124, was
published at $3,630,000 and later reduced to $1,402,122.87 by an amendment
that the notice does not explain. The ACIC is not on the FOI Act's list of
exempt agencies (SRC_FOI_ACT_1982), so it can be asked.

This is the route for RG-093, which asks whether any Australian
law-enforcement body runs Palantir software and for what. The project's rule
stands until a record answers it: no row links Palantir to an Australian
policing capability without a primary source.

Expect claims under section 37 (law enforcement) and section 33 (security).
The request asks for the name and purpose of a system, not for its contents,
targets or methods, so an edited copy should be possible.

## Draft request text

> I request access under the *Freedom of Information Act 1982* to the
> following documents of the Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission (and
> of its predecessor, the Australian Crime Commission) about its contracts
> with Palantir Technologies Inc. and Palantir Technologies Australia Pty Ltd
> published on AusTender as CN2386341, CN2514842, CN3287309, CN3557030 and
> CN3996124, including amendments.
>
> 1. The statement of work, statement of requirement or equivalent schedule
>    for each contract, to the extent that it names the system or systems the
>    software supports and describes their business purpose.
> 2. The document recording the reason for amendment A1 to CN3996124, which
>    reduced the contract's value.
> 3. Any document stating where data processed by the Palantir software is
>    hosted: in Australia or elsewhere, and on ACIC premises or in a
>    commercial cloud.
> 4. Any privacy impact assessment for a system that uses Palantir software,
>    or a document recording that none was done.
>
> I do not seek operational, intelligence or investigative information, the
> contents of any database, or personal information; names of officers below
> Senior Executive Service level and of supplier staff may be removed. An
> edited copy is acceptable.
>
> I ask that any charge be waived because giving access is in the general
> public interest: whether a national criminal intelligence agency uses
> software from a supplier whose policing work overseas is a matter of public
> controversy is a question the public cannot answer from the contract
> notices.
>
> I would prefer the documents electronically.
