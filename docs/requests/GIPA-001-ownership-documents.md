# GIPA-001: ownership documents on NSW data centre applications

Status: **drafted**, 2026-10-22. Not lodged.

To: NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure, under the
Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009.
Lodge through the Department's GIPA access application page:
https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/department-of-planning-housing-and-infrastructure/information-access-governance-and-feedback/right-to-information/gipa-act-access-application

Cost: $30 application fee. Ask for the 50% processing-charge reduction on the
ground of special benefit to the public (below).

## Why this request

On 2026-10-21 the observatory tried to read the ownership documents filed with
eleven State Significant Development applications for data centres. Thirteen of
seventeen answered 401 on the NSW Planning Portal. They are the documents that
say who owns the land and who stands behind the applicant. In nine of the
Department's own assessment reports, the applicant is described as acting for
a data centre operator the report does not name. The record of what the
public was consulted on is incomplete without them.

> I do not seek personal information. I am content for the names, signatures
> and contact details of individuals to be redacted, and ask that company names,
> ACNs and the identity of any company on whose behalf an applicant acts be

> large public infrastructure decisions, several already determined, and the
> documents identify who holds the land and the consents. Release would let the
> public see who is behind applications the Department has assessed.
>
> I would prefer the documents electronically.
