# GIPA-003: warning letters, a donation disclosure and the August 2026 delegation, from the Department of Planning

Status: **drafted**, 2026-10-30. Not lodged.

To: NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure, under the
Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009.
Lodge through the Department's GIPA access application page:
https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/department-of-planning-housing-and-infrastructure/information-access-governance-and-feedback/right-to-information/gipa-act-access-application

Cost: $30 application fee. Ask for the 50% processing-charge reduction on the
ground of special benefit to the public (below). The Department should decide
within 20 working days.

It can be split into three applications ($30 each) if the Department says the
scope is too wide. Keep part 1 first.

## Why this request

Three things in the NSW data centre record cannot be explained from what is
public:

1. **Two warning letters, both locked.** The Roberts Road Data Centre record
   (SSD-10330, CDC) lists "CDC Data Centres response warning letter". The
   Station Road Data Centre Expansion record (SSD-33781208) lists a warning
   letter dated 29 May 2024 and a Department email about it. All of them, and
   a "scanned signed letter" on Roberts Road, are restricted on the Planning
   Portal (RG-058).
2. **A disclosure that did not change the decision-maker.** The Roberts Road
   record holds a political donations disclosure statement, signed on 2
   December 2019 and filed with the EIS. It declares a $7,500 donation on 18
   June 2018. The Department's assessment report says the 9 March 2020
   delegation applied where "a political disclosure statement has not been
   made", that no reportable political donations were made by the Applicant in
   the last two years, and that the Executive Director could decide under
   delegation (RG-069). No improper conduct is alleged. The question is how
   the disclosure was assessed.
3. **The delegation of 18 August 2026.** The assessment reports for Project
   Apollo (SSD-74069708) and Glendenning Road (SSD-73761707) quote a
   delegation for "relevant resource, energy or data centre projects". Its
   conditions, as quoted, do not mention a council objection, unlike the 2020
   and 2022 delegations. Project Apollo was approved under it while the City of
   Ryde maintained its objection. The Planning Portal still says a council
   objection generally makes the Independent Planning Commission the consent
   authority (RG-040).

## Draft application text

> I apply under the *Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009* for
> access to the following government information held by the Department.
>
> **Part 1: warning letters on data centre consents**
>
> 1. The warning letter issued in relation to the Roberts Road Data Centre
>    (SSD-10330) and CDC Data Centres' response, listed on the NSW Planning
>    Portal as "CDC Data Centres response warning letter", and the document
>    listed as "scanned signed letter ssd 10330".
> 2. The warning letter dated 29 May 2024 in relation to the Station Road
>    Data Centre Expansion (SSD-33781208), the Department email about it
>    listed on the Portal, and any response from the consent holder.
> 3. A list, or a document from which one can be produced, of every warning
>    letter, penalty notice, official caution, direction or order the
>    Department has issued in relation to a development consent for a data
>    centre (data storage premises) since 1 January 2019, with the consent
>    number, date and the condition concerned.
>
> **Part 2: the Roberts Road political donation disclosure**
>
> 4. Any record of the Department's consideration of the political donations
>    disclosure statement filed on SSD-10330 (signed 2 December 2019) when
>    deciding whether the application could be determined under the delegation
>    of 9 March 2020, including any file note, email or advice.
>
> **Part 3: the delegation of 18 August 2026**
>
> 5. The instrument of delegation of 18 August 2026 by which the Minister
>    delegated the determination of State significant development applications
>    for resource, energy or data centre projects, and any brief to the Minister
>    recommending it.
> 6. Any document stating how the Independent Planning Commission's role as
>    consent authority, where a council has objected, applies to applications
>    determined under that delegation, including any related amendment to
>    State Environmental Planning Policy (Planning Systems) 2021.
>
> I do not seek personal information about any individual. Names of officers
> below Senior Executive Service level, and any personal details in
> correspondence, may be removed. An edited copy is acceptable.
>
> I ask for a 50% reduction in processing charges because the information is
> of special benefit to the public generally: data centres are the subject of
> a NSW Legislative Council inquiry, and whether consent conditions are
> enforced, and who decides applications that a council opposes, are matters
> of public interest.
>
> I would prefer the information electronically.
