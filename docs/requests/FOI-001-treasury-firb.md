# FOI-001: foreign investment decisions on data centres, from the Treasury

Status: **drafted**, 2026-10-22. Not lodged.

To: the Department of the Treasury, under the Freedom of Information Act 1982
(Cth). Lodge with the request form on Treasury's FOI page,
https://treasury.gov.au/the-department/accountability-reporting/foi, or by
email to FOI@treasury.gov.au.

Cost: no application fee. Processing charges may apply to documents that are
not your personal information; ask for them to be waived in the public
interest (below). Treasury must acknowledge within 14 days and decide within
30, plus 30 more if it consults third parties.

## Why this request

The Foreign Investment Review Board publishes counts, not names. Its report for
January to March 2026 approved 311 commercial proposals, 97 with conditions,
and suppresses "Any data that may enable the identification of a particular
person or organisation". The words "data centre" do not appear (RG-006). The
largest data centre sale in Australia, AirTrunk to Blackstone and CPP
Investments, needed foreign investment approval, and its conditions are not
public. The text below is template 5 of
`data-pipeline/reports/RG007_foi_strategy.md`, narrowed so Treasury can find
the documents without an unmanageable search.

Expect exemptions: commercial information, national security and foreign
relations. A decision that sets out which exemption covers which document is
itself a record of what the public is not told, and can go to review.

## Draft request text

> I request access under the *Freedom of Information Act 1982* to the
> following documents, for the period 1 January 2020 to the date of this
> request.
>
> 1. The decision, statement of reasons and any conditions imposed on each
>    foreign investment proposal, or national security notification, whose
>    target was a business that owns or operates a data centre in Australia,
>    including the acquisition of AirTrunk completed in December 2024.
> 2. Any standard conditions, template or guidance used by the Treasury or the
>    Foreign Investment Review Board for data centre or data storage
>    acquisitions, including conditions about where data is held or who may
>    access it.
> 3. A count, by financial year and outcome (approved, approved with
>    conditions, refused, withdrawn), of proposals whose target was a data
>    centre business, if such a count exists as a document.
>
> I do not seek personal information about any individual; names of officers
> below Senior Executive Service level may be removed.
>
> I ask that any charge be waived because giving access is in the general
> public interest: data centres are critical infrastructure under the
> *Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018*, and both the NSW Legislative
> Council and the Senate are inquiring into them.
>
> I would prefer the documents electronically.

## What each item would answer

| Item | Answers | Card it would unlock |
