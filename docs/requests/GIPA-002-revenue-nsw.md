# GIPA-002: tax concessions for data centres, from Revenue NSW

Status: **drafted**, 2026-10-22. Not lodged.

To: the NSW Department of Customer Service, which holds Revenue NSW's
information, under the Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009.
Lodge through the Department's GIPA application page:
https://nsw.gov.au/customer-service/who-we-are/access-to-information/gipa-act-application
(help: gipa@customerservice.nsw.gov.au).

Cost: $30 application fee, paid by EFT. Processing may be charged at $30 an
hour; ask for the 50% reduction on the ground of special benefit to the public
(below).

## Why this request

The industry has told the NSW inquiry that "Data centre operators do not
receive subsidies or tax incentives" (1 May 2026), and the Business Council's
submission says the same. The head of the Premier's Department said the
government does not "contemplate" taking part financially (22 May 2026). No
revenue officer has been asked, and no published instrument says either way
(RG-007). This request asks for the instruments and the counts, never a named
taxpayer: a request naming a taxpayer would be refused for confidentiality and
waste the attempt. A "no such information" answer is a finding too, and the
observatory will record it as one.

The text below is template 1 of `data-pipeline/reports/RG007_foi_strategy.md`,
tightened. The Victorian, Queensland and Department of Planning templates stay
in that file for later.

## Draft application text

> I apply under the *Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009* for
> access to the following information held by Revenue NSW, for the period
> 1 January 2019 to the date of this application.
>
> 1. Any guideline, policy, ruling, Commissioner's determination, practice
>    note, internal instruction or eligibility criterion under which a payroll
>    tax exemption, reduction, deferral or refund has been or may be granted in
>    respect of a data centre, a data storage facility, or a development
>    described as "data storage" under the planning legislation.
> 2. Any such document under which land tax, surcharge land tax or duty has
>    been or may be exempted, reduced, deferred or refunded in respect of land
>    used, or proposed to be used, for a data centre.
> 3. For each financial year from 2019-20 to 2025-26, the number of entities
>    granted any payroll tax, land tax, surcharge or duty concession in respect
>    of a facility whose principal activity is data storage or processing, and
>    the total value of those concessions. I do not seek the identity of any
>    taxpayer or any individual assessment.
> 4. Any submission, advice or briefing Revenue NSW prepared for the NSW Data
>    Centre Consultation Paper (2026) or the NSW Data Centre Guidelines (August
>    2026) on the tax or duty treatment of data centres.
>
> If no information is held for an item, I ask that the decision say so for
> that item.
>
> I ask for the 50% reduction in processing charges under section 66 of the
> Act. The information is of special benefit to the public generally: whether
> the State gives tax concessions to a fast-growing industry is before a
> Legislative Council inquiry, and the public record holds only the industry's
> statement that it receives none.
>
> I would prefer the information electronically.
