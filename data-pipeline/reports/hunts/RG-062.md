# RG-062: Has any consent relied on the Guidelines' recycled-water definition?

Question: Establish whether any consent or modification has relied on the Guidelines' definition of recycled water as including on-site treatment 'such as from captured stormwater' to satisfy the recycled-water expectation.
Status: resolved, as at 2026-11-18.

## Batch 8 (2026-11-18)

Status after this pass: **resolved**. Pack: `data/packs/batch8_open_questions.json` (scripts/curate_batch8.py). Sources quoted: SRC_NSWGUIDE26, SRC_NSWPORTAL_CONSENTS, SRC_SSD73761707_ASSESSMENT, SRC_SSD74069708_ASSESSMENT.

UPDATED 2026-11-18 (research batch 8): RESOLVED (negative). The NSW Data Centre Guidelines define the term: 'Recycled water includes that provided by recycled water networks or on-site water treatment, such as from captured stormwater.' Read against all 20 archived consent instruments and the modification reports read in batch 7, no consent or modification cites that definition or relies on it to meet a recycled-water expectation. Only two assessment reports mention the Guidelines (Glendenning Road and Project Apollo, both after their release on 17 August 2026); each says 'The Department has considered the Guidelines as a relevant consideration under section 4.15 of the EP&A Act' and lists the related conditions as renewable power purchase, retrofit capability, PUE and WUE monitoring through a Sustainability Management Plan, and amenity; recycled water is not among them. The closest usage predates the Guidelines: the DigiCo SYD1 consent (December 2025) lists, under 'Recycled water and reuse, through:', the 'Use of 2x5000L existing rainwater tanks', rainwater counted as recycled water, which is the reading the later definition allows. The three consents decided since July 2026 (Project Pluto, Project Apollo, Glendenning Road) instead require a 'review of available water servicing arrangements, in consultation with the relevant water service provider, to identify any alternative non-potable or recycled water supply options'. Re-open if a Sustainability Management Plan approved under one of them relies on the definition.
