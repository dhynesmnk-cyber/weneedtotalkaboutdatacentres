# RG-105: Road 1 and 1-5 Khartoum Road — one campus or two records?

Question: are Road 1 (`SSD-80814238`, `SITE_ROAD_1`) and 1-5 Khartoum Road (`SSD-63235720`,
`SITE_KHARTOUM`) two buildings on one Stockland M_Park campus, or one project recorded twice?
Status: **open**, opened 2026-11-15 while implementing the author's acceptance of RG-099.

## Why it opened

Both portal records give the **same address** — "1-5 Khartoum Road, Macquarie Park" — and both
documents place the development on the **same lot**, Part Lot 2 DP 1043041. RG-084 removed one
duplicate Macquarie Park row on exactly this kind of evidence, so the coincidence had to be
recorded rather than passed over.

## Log

- 2026-11-15: reading Road 1's EIS for RG-099's capacity label. Its glossary defines
  **Site: "Part of Lot 2 Deposited Plan 1043041"**, and p11 places the site "within the broader
  'MPark' Stockland landholding".
- 2026-11-15: the EIS's **Table 5** (p22) states: "Site Name **MPark Road 1 Data Centre** / Land
  ownership **The land is owned by Stockland.** / Address **1-5 Khartoum Road, Macquarie Park** /
  Legal Description (Title Particulars) **Part Lot 2 DP 1043041**".
- 2026-11-15: the neighbouring consent's Schedule 1, archived: "Application Number:
  **SSD-63235720** Applicant: **Stockland Development Pty Limited** … Site: **1-5 Khartoum Road,
  Macquarie Park Part Lot 2 DP 1043041**". Same lot, same address, different case.
- 2026-11-15: the portal nodes compared. `road-1-data-centre` address and site name are both
  "1-5 Khartoum Road, Macquarie Park"; `1-5-khartoum-road-data-centre` has the same address and
  the site name **"M_Park"**.
- 2026-11-15: `data/packs/site_portal_fields.json` fills both rows with the same address, which
  is what surfaced this.

## What is NOT assumed

- **Not merged.** They are two consents at different stages with different figures:
  `SSD-63235720` **approved 11 March 2026**, 76.4 MW maximum power consumption;
  `SSD-80814238` **in Assessment** (More Information Required on 2026-11-16), 34.3 MW **IT load**
  (settled by RG-099 on the EIS's own label at p35).
- **Not summed.** Until this is settled, any capacity total across the two rows double-counts if
  they are stages of one campus.
- **No owner assigned.** The EIS says "Stockland" without naming an entity; the record holds both
  Stockland Development Pty Limited and Stockland Trust Management Limited, and the consent
  applicant for Road 1 is Stockland Development Pty Limited per the portal. `owner_id` stays unset.

## Refused or restricted

- Nothing refused for this question: both documents are archived and were read offline.
- Context: the portal's attachment JSON route answered 401 for every candidate on 2026-11-16, so
  a staging plan or site plan that is not already archived cannot be fetched.

## Leads (not facts)

- The name "Road 1" reads as an internal road within the M_Park estate rather than a street
  address, which would make it a building label on one campus. Nothing read here states that.
- Stockland's M_Park project pages and ASX announcements would name the buildings and their
  stages. Not fetched.
