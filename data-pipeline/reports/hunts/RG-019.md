# RG-019: Synchronous condensers serving a data centre corridor

Question: Establish the existence and location of any synchronous condenser installation serving an Australian data centre corridor.
Status: resolved, as at 2026-11-18.

## Batch 8, second pass (2026-11-18)

Status after this pass: **resolved**. Pack: `data/packs/batch8b_open_questions.json` (scripts/curate_batch8b.py). Sources quoted: SRC_TRANSGRID_SYSTEM_STRENGTH, SRC_TRANSGRID_SYSTEM_STRENGTH_FAQ.

UPDATED 2026-11-18 (research batch 8, second pass): RESOLVED. One exists in plan, none in service. Transgrid's System Strength Project, directed by the NSW Government, will 'fast-track the deployment of synchronous condensers at five strategic locations', 'including Kemps Creek (Western Sydney)': two smaller machines at the 'Kemps Creek 500kV substation, off Gurner Avenue, West Hoxton in Western Sydney', the substation of the Kemps Creek - Mamre Road data centre corridor. 'Progressive energisation of synchronous condensers at the five sites is expected from early 2028 to early 2029.' Neither Transgrid document names a data centre; the condensers serve the network, not a customer. Re-open if a data centre's own connection includes one.
