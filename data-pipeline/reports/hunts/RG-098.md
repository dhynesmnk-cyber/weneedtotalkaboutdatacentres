# RG-098

Question: Is AirTrunk SYD2 the same facility as the Lane Cove West Data Centre (SSD-9741, 1 Sirius Road), and is the Apollo Place Data Centre (SSD-67407231) AirTrunk's?

## Batch 7 (2026-11-18)

Status after this pass: **in_progress**. Pack: `data/packs/batch7_open_questions.json` (scripts/curate_batch7.py). Sources quoted: SRC_OP_AIRTRUNK_SYD2_SYDNEY_NORTH.

ANSWERED FROM THE OPERATOR'S OWN PAGE; THE RECORD CHANGE IS THE AUTHOR'S. AirTrunk's SYD2 page credits Greenbox Architecture, SSD-9741's applicant, with delivering SYD2 'in collaboration with AirTrunk', dates it '2019 - ongoing' (SSD-9741 was determined on 15 November 2019, and its report described a client with 'two data centres in Melbourne and western Sydney'), places it at 1 Sirius Road and links to the planning portal; and says the Apollo Place data centre (SSD-67407231) 'has been located adjacent to AirTrunk's existing facility at 1 Sirius Road' and 'is Phase 5 of AirTrunk's investment within the precinct'. PROPOSED for the author: (1) SITE_LANE_COVE_WEST is the consent for the facility SITE_AIRTRUNK_SYD2 records, so the two are one site, as RG-084 found for Macquarie Park; (2) SITE_APOLLO_PLACE's operator is AirTrunk, REPORTED on the operator's own statement. Neither change is made by this pack.

## Decided by the author (2026-11-18)

Status: **resolved**. Pack: `data/packs/batch7_decisions.json` (scripts/curate_batch7_decisions.py).

Both proposals accepted: SITE_LANE_COVE_WEST is now the SYD2 record (operator AirTrunk, operational; SITE_AIRTRUNK_SYD2 retired), and Apollo Place's operator is AirTrunk, REPORTED.
