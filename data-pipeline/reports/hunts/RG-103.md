# RG-103: addresses and capacities for the eight new register rows

Question: addresses, site areas and capacities for the eight facilities added from the HCF
register (ADC Mitchell and Fyshwick, NEXTDC B1 and B2, Fujitsu Western Sydney and Homebush Bay,
Polaris, Macquarie IC3 East), and which legal entity operates Polaris?
Status: open, 2026-11-15.

## Log
- 2026-11-15: the eight rows were created from operators' own pages (see RG-101 for the fetches
  and SHA-256s). What each page states, and what it does not:
  - **ADC** (`f8df92e8`, `09f0922f`): names Mitchell and Fyshwick, says both are operational,
    gives a Canberra phone number. No address, no site area, no capacity for either facility.
  - **NEXTDC B1** (`2f4b5892`): 2.25 MW labelled IT Capacity; 1,650 m² in the portfolio table.
    No street address.
  - **NEXTDC B2** (`7658a83e`): 12 MW labelled IT Capacity; 6,000 m². The FY26 results
    presentation gives "6MW built, 2MW in progress" against "Total power planned 12MW", so the
    stored 12 MW is the facility's IT capacity, not delivered capacity. No street address.
  - **Fujitsu** (`102b2062`, 30 September 2020): places Western Sydney and Homebush Bay in NSW
    and gives "a combined total capacity of 90 megawatts at the Western Sydney data centre
    campus" as the end-state of a phased expansion. Six years old, and a campus total: quoted,
    not stored. No suburb or address for either facility.
  - **Polaris** (`92095dd1`): address 11 Mary Mackenroth Lane, Springfield Central QLD 4300;
    "over 14,500m2 over 5 levels, with over 7,000m2 of raised technical floor space" — both lower
    bounds, and technical space is not gross floor area. No capacity.
  - **Macquarie IC3 East** (`15404f06`): 12 MW IT Load, 11,700 m² technical space, within a
    "65MW" campus. No street address on the page.
- 2026-11-15: no lower bound and no technical-space figure was stored as a value; every one is
  quoted in `source_refs` instead.

## Refused or restricted

## Leads (not facts)
- Third-party directories give addresses for ADC Mitchell (38 Vicars Street) and the NEXTDC
  Brisbane facilities. Not used: an address is a locatable fact and needs the operator, a
  planning record or a register.
- The HCF register names **Springfield Real Estate Sales Pty Ltd** as Polaris's certified
  provider; Polaris's page names the **Springfield City Group** as 100% owner. An ABR/ASIC lookup
  would settle the relationship. Search summaries also mention a cancelled "Polaris Data Centre
  Unit Trust & Springfield Real Estate Sales Pty Ltd" ABN — a lead, not a finding.
