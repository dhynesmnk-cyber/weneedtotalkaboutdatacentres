# RG-012: Anthropic and the Western Downs Digital Park

Question: Confirm the Anthropic / Western Downs Digital Park relationship from a primary source, and obtain the Zerra DC development application.
Status: resolved, as at 2026-10-22. Batch 4 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line C3).

**Conflict of interest.** Anthropic is the company whose model drafts this research. Every finding below rests on documents that are archived and cited; the author should read them directly before the card is approved.

## Log
- 2026-09-18 (earlier): the lodged development application (SRC_WDDP_TPR, Urbis Town Planning Report V4) obtained and read. It names WDDP Pty Ltd, and neither Anthropic nor Zerra.
- 2026-10-22: Dexus's ASX release of 16 September 2026, read from the EQS regulatory distribution copy (`data/raw/operators/dexus/`, sha256 `435642da`) because ASX's terms limit reuse of its own copies. Authorised by Dexus's General Counsel:
  - Australian Data Centres, 85% owned by Dexus, holds 25% of a consortium with Zerra DC and Macquarie Capital "in connection with the proposed development of a hyperscale data centre campus for an Australian subsidiary of Anthropic in the Western Downs region of Queensland".
  - "The Consortium has entered into lease documentation with the Australian subsidiary of Anthropic for the delivery of the first stage of the campus, subject to customary approvals."
  - The release's title names the Western Downs Digital Park.
- 2026-10-22: iTnews' report (17 Sep 2026) is a teaser for a Reuters story ("two people familiar with the deal said"); not kept.
- 2026-10-22: Anthropic's own news page holds no announcement found by search. The Queensland Premier's statement to parliament: Hansard's robots.txt is refused (`data/raw/registers/qld_hansard/refused.json`).

## Leads (not facts)
- Search summaries say Anthropic told the ABC it would use the campus to answer Claude users' questions, not to train models, and that the deal needs FIRB and council approval.

Resolved: a party to the deal confirms, in a regulated disclosure, that the consortium has entered lease documentation with Anthropic's Australian subsidiary, subject to approvals.
