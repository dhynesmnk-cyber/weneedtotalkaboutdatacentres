Carrier DB1; DigiCo BNE2, ADL1 and SYD1; and Vocus TransACT House Dickson — who operates them, and
what are their status and capacity?

2026-11-15; three of the remaining five (DigiCo SYD1, ADL1, BNE2) were placed on 2026-11-17 from DigiCo's
own ASX documents, supplied by the author. Two are still held: Cloud Carrier DB1 and Vocus TransACT House.
