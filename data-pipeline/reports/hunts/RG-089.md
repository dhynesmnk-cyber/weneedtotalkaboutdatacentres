# RG-089: The ASD clearance discrepancy

Question: Resolve the ASD clearance discrepancy: Crikey (17 Feb 2026) says ASD granted Palantir "a top security clearance" in November 2024; Palantir's announcement of IRAP PROTECTED is 20 November 2025. What was granted or assessed, and how do any ASD certification, the IRAP assessment and facility or personnel clearances relate?
Status: in_progress, as at 2026-10-22. Batch 4 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line C3).

## Log
- 2026-10-22: the FOI Act (archived at `data/raw/registers/legislation/`, sha256 `4f00f6ca`) lists ASD in Schedule 2, Part I, Division 1 as an exempt agency. Section 7(1) deems the bodies listed there not to be prescribed authorities, and section 7(2A) exempts any agency from the Act for documents that originated with or were received from ASD. The FOI request to ASD that the plan proposed cannot be made.
- 2026-10-22: no ASD-published list of assessed or certified services was reachable (cyber.gov.au closed the connection). The Hosting Certification Framework register (`data/raw/hcf/`) does not list Palantir.
- 2026-10-22: what Defence makes itself is reachable. Security clearances for companies that work for Defence run through the Defence Industry Security Program, which Defence administers. FOI-003 (`docs/requests/FOI-003-defence-palantir-clearance.md`) asks Defence for its own records of any program membership or facility clearance held by Palantir Technologies Australia from 2024 to 2025, and of any Defence review of Palantir's IRAP assessment. It excludes documents received from ASD so the request is not refused whole.
- 2026-10-22: a right-of-reply question to Crikey about the source of "a top security clearance" is listed in FOI-003's notes for the author to send.

## Refused or restricted

- ASD: outside the FOI Act by statute.
