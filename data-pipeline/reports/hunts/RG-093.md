# RG-093: Palantir and Australian law enforcement

Question: Establish whether any Australian law-enforcement or criminal-justice agency runs Palantir software, and for what: the ACIC contract's purpose; any state police, Home Affairs/ABF or AFP deployment; the vendor of the WA Police real-time facial recognition trial; NSW Police tooling.
Status: in_progress, as at 2026-10-22. Batch 4 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line C3).

## Log
- 2026-10-22: WA Police's own Privacy Impact Assessment for "Overt Live Facial Recognition" (cover dated November 2025, version 1.0 of 10 May 2026; `data/raw/operators/wapolice/`, sha256 `9a8eee4a`; PDF not committed). It names the vendor as NEC and the technology, if endorsed, as NEC's NeoFace Watch using the NeoFace M40 algorithm, "used by the Metropolitan Police". It says "Data stored in Australia" and that non-matched biometrics are not retained. It does not mention Palantir. It names three police officers; they are recorded by role only.
- 2026-10-22: the Commonwealth ledger (RG-086). The ACIC has bought Palantir software since 2014 (5 notices, $6,068,875.14) and AUSTRAC since 2016 (6 notices, $28,117,548.41). No AFP or Home Affairs notice names Palantir. No notice says what the software is used for.
- 2026-10-22: the FOI Act lets the ACIC be asked: it is not in Schedule 2. AUSTRAC's intelligence documents are exempt (s 7(2G)), but not its contracts. FOI-004 to the ACIC drafted (`docs/requests/FOI-004-acic-palantir.md`).
- 2026-10-22: state police and justice departments: the state tender portals refused (RG-086).

## Refused or restricted
- State tender portals: Victoria, South Australia and the ACT (HTTP 403), WA (robots.txt refused); buy.nsw (batch 3).

## Leads (not facts)
- iTnews (4 Sep 2026) states "Australia's Department of Defence uses Palantir's Maven smart system to inform targeting solutions", without a source. No contract notice or Defence record read says so. It stays a lead.
- Search summaries say ABC News reported the NeoFace M40 product. The PIA itself says so, so the summary is not needed.

## Outcome
Metrics 338 and 339 now name the vendor, NEC, from WA Police's own document. The separation rule stands: no row links Palantir to an Australian policing capability. FOI-004 asks the ACIC what its Palantir software supports.
