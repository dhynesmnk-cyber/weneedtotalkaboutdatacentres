# RG-007: State tax and land concessions to data centre proponents

Question: Establish whether any Australian state has granted payroll tax exemptions, land tax concessions, stamp duty relief or concessional leasehold land to a data centre proponent, and on what conditions.
Status: in_progress, as at 2026-10-22. Batch 3 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line M3).

## Log

The record holds the industry's and the government's word that there are no concessions, and no revenue office's. In-kind support is documented instead (RG-063). GIPA-002 is ready to lodge; the Victorian, Queensland and Commonwealth templates stay in the strategy file.
