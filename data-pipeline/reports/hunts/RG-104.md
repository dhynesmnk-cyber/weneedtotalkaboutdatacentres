# RG-104: IC3 Super West — operational, or under construction?

Question: is `SITE_MACQ_IC3` — recorded as "IC3 Super West", operational, from a grade-C
directory — the register's certified "IC3", when Macquarie's own page says IC3 Super West is
under construction with 47 MW of IT load?
Status: open, 2026-11-15.

  and IC3e" and added the register as a source. The match is on the name "IC3"; the register does
