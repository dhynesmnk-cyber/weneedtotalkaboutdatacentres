# RG-095: Is Coles leaving Palantir, and why?

Question: Is Coles' Palantir arrangement still current? Establish from a primary source whether Coles announced in early September 2026 that it will not extend the arrangement beyond 2027, and what reason Coles itself gave.
Status: resolved, as at 2026-10-22. Batch 4 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line C3).

## Log
- 2026-10-22: archived three reports (`data/raw/operators/coles/`):
  - iTnews, 4 Sep 2026 (sha256 `97e4a218`). A Coles spokesperson: "Coles and Palantir have agreed to conclude their partnership after three years of collaboration." The timing is "yet to be settled, but the arrangement is not expected to run beyond calendar 2027." Coles says the software "is deployed within Coles' own environment" and "Palantir does not control the data, nor can it independently repurpose it". GetUp claimed a win after 86,000 signatures and a push for a shareholder vote. The Sydney Morning Herald reported it first.
  - Information Age (ACS), 7 Sep 2026 (sha256 `c2a5bd00`). The same statement. Coles "denied that Palantir had access to CCTV, facial recognition or any of its crime-prevention measures". GetUp's email to supporters: "It's only in the last few weeks, with the shareholder vote around the corner adding yet another layer of pressure, that Coles changed tack."
  - Cyber Daily, 4 Sep 2026 (sha256 `91dd1c91`). The same statement, given to Cyber Daily. GetUp's statement includes "Palantir still has lucrative contracts with Westpac, Rio Tinto, and a host of government agencies" (see RG-088).
- 2026-10-22: Coles' own channels. The media releases page redirects to a news page with no Palantir item. The 2026 Notice of Annual General Meeting (15 September 2026; meeting 6 November; sha256 `30f1a380`, PDF not committed) has five items of business, none about Palantir, and the word does not appear. GetUp's shareholder vote is not on it.
- 2026-10-22: no Coles ASX announcement was sought: ASX's terms limit reuse of its copies, and nothing points to one.
