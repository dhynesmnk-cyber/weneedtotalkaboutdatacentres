# RG-075: Who peer-reviews the technical evidence, and how often

Question: Do peer reviews of acoustic and CFD evidence ever review work by the same firm, and how often is peer review commissioned at all?
Status: resolved, as at 2026-11-18.

## Batch 8 (2026-11-18)

Status after this pass: **resolved**. Pack: `data/packs/batch8_open_questions.json` (scripts/curate_batch8.py). Sources quoted: SRC_SSD101067971_GHG_REVIEW, SRC_SSD10330_MOD2_GHG_REVIEW, SRC_SSD10330_MOD4_GHG_REVIEW, SRC_SSD69637456_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW, SRC_SSD9741_MOD3_ACOUSTIC_PEER_REVIEW.

RESOLVED for the documents on the public record. Six of the 63 projects in the attachment index carry a review document by title: two acoustic peer reviews (DigiCo SYD1; Lane Cove West Mod 3); an acoustic and an air quality 'review statement' on 51 Huntingwood Drive Mod 1, a withdrawn modification (the acoustic one answered 401, not public; refusal recorded); and three greenhouse gas reviews (Project Atlas; Roberts Road Mods 2 and 4). OPENED: at DigiCo SYD1, 'Renzo Tonin & Associates (RTA) was engaged by Patch Planning, on behalf of HDI SYD1 Property Holdings Pty Limited (DigiCo) to peer review the noise and vibration impact assessment (NVIA) report undertaken by Pulse White Noise Acoustics (PWNA)', across 'reviews or partial reviews of eight revisions'. At Lane Cove West Mod 3 the same reviewer 'was engaged by EMKC Cubed Management (EMKC) to undertake an independent review of the Noise Impact Assessment (NIA) prepared by Pulse White Noise Acoustics (PWNA)', in 'seven reviews of the NIA'. Both letters say 'The peer review process did not involve any detailed modelling checks or remodelling by RTA.' At Roberts Road Mod 4, 'Zephyr Environmental Pty Ltd (Zephyr) has been engaged by Canberra Data Centres (CDC) to perform an independent review' of SLR Consulting's greenhouse gas report ('Revision 05, SLR Consulting, 16 July 2026'); under the EPA's Guide for Large Emitters, above 100,000 tonnes a year 'mitigation assessments must be verified by an independent expert review'. All three greenhouse reviews are by the same firm, Zephyr Environmental: Roberts Road Mod 2 ('engaged by Urbis, on behalf of Canberra Data Centres (CDC)', reviewing SLR's report) and Project Atlas, where 'The Assessment Report has been prepared by LCI on behalf of Goodman Property Services' and the reviewer 'has been engaged by LCI Consultants (LCI ), on behalf of Goodman Property Services': there the author of the report engaged its reviewer. The Mod 2 and Atlas letters each say 'No actual or perceived conflicts of interest have come to my attention, and I have not been employed by the proponent within the last five years'; the Mod 4 letter, written after the Mod 2 engagement for the same proponent, carries no such statement. Each review found the work 'adequate' or 'satisfactory'. ANSWER: in every review opened the reviewer is a different firm from the author, so no firm was found reviewing its own work; every review was engaged by the applicant, its planner or the report's author, none by the Department; the acoustic reviews checked by comment and meeting, not by re-running the model. The greenhouse reviews follow the EPA's rule; nothing read requires the acoustic ones.
