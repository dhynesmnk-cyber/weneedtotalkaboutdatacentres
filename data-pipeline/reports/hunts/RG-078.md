# RG-078: Who commissioned the phantom-demand research

Question: Who commissioned and paid for the Oxford Economics research on phantom data centre demand, cited by both the BCA and Data Centres Australia?
Status: resolved, 2026-10-22. Batch 3 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line M4).

## Log
- 2026-10-22: Oxford Economics' page for the briefing archived (`data/raw/operators/oxfordeconomics/`, sha256 `4985ce50`): "Estimating Data Centre 'Phantom Demand'", 21 November 2025, subtitled "Surging data centre connection requests drive phantom demand for power" (the title the BCA's submission cites).
- 2026-10-22: it says AEMO "commissioned Oxford Economics to develop independent forecasts of electricity consumption by data centres", and ends: "This research briefing was commissioned by AWS. The analysis and findings are Oxford Economics."
- 2026-10-22: the NSW Data Centre Guidelines (August 2026) introduce the firm as the one "which prepared AEMO's forecast on data centres" and cite the briefing by title in footnote 4. They do not mention AWS.
- 2026-10-22: the inquiry Chair, 22 May: "Data Centres Australia and the Business Council of Australia have both cited Oxford Economics phantom demand research".
- 2026-10-22: corrections: lobbying row 6 named the BCA as commissioner; metrics 26 and 290 to 292 said "for AEMO" or "for the BCA".
