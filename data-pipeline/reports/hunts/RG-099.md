# RG-099: capacity the planning portal does not label

Question: for the NSW State Significant Development sites whose capacity rests
only on the portal's one-line description, establish from the project's own
planning document whether the figure is IT load or total site draw.
Status: **resolved 2026-11-15, author accepted and loaded** — three of the five
settled from documents, two cannot be settled from any public document and no
longer carry a figure in any capacity column (see Outcome). Loaded by
`scripts/curate_rg061_rg099.py`.

Two things were decided at load time that the findings table below only proposed:
- **`max_capacity_mw` was cleared as well as `it_capacity_mw`** on Turner Road and
  O'Riordan. `headline_mw` is `COALESCE(max_capacity_mw, total_capacity_mw,
  it_capacity_mw)`, so clearing the IT column alone would have left the same
  unlabelled number as the row's public headline. This follows the treatment
  already applied to NEXTDC S7 on 2026-10-14.
- **Road 1's `max_capacity_mw` was NOT raised to 42 MW.** p54's figure is the
  electrical infrastructure's design ceiling for *critical IT load*, and
  `max_capacity_mw` is the full-campus-build-out ceiling the headline reads first.
  Storing it would publish an IT-side ceiling as this site's headline against other
  sites' total-power maxima. The passage is quoted on the row instead.

*Reconstructed on 2026-10-21 from the archives, `scripts/curate_rg002.py`,
`scripts/curate_ssd_documents.py` and the working session.*
