# RG-044

Question: Assess Unions NSW's proposal for local content and apprentice quotas at no less than Renewable Energy Sector Board standards, and its warning that a market-based renewable funding mechanism would be opaque and would prevent tracking local content.

## Batch 7 (2026-11-18)

Status after this pass: **resolved**. Pack: `data/packs/batch7_open_questions.json` (scripts/curate_batch7.py). Sources quoted: SRC_INQ_T_0522, SRC_NSWGUIDE26.

RESOLVED. The proposal, in the corrected transcript of 22 May 2026, from Unions NSW's research and policy officer (by role): 'Our recommendation is to require local content no less than that set by the Renewable Energy Sector Board, both for data centres and the associated renewable energy', and, of a market-based renewable funding model, 'a risk ... of it being opaque' so local content could not be tracked. Assessed against what was adopted: the NSW Data Centre Guidelines (August 2026), performance measure 16, say only that data centres 'are encouraged to prioritise use of local content where possible'; apprenticeship targets appear as things developers 'could' adopt with unions. None of the 20 archived consent instruments mentions local content. So the proposal was not adopted as a requirement, in the guidelines or in any consent read. Whether a market mechanism would be opaque is a prediction the record cannot test.
