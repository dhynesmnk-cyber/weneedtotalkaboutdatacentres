# RG-087: Where Australian data sits, per Palantir deployment

Question: Establish where Australian data actually sits for each Palantir deployment: which entity, which platform (Palantir Platform Australia on AWS Australian regions vs on-premises vs offshore), and whether any workload leaves Australian jurisdiction. Map any identified hosting facilities against the site register.
Status: in_progress, as at 2026-10-22. Batch 4 of docs/RESEARCH-PLAN.md (story line C3).

## Log
- 2026-10-22: re-read what is already held. Palantir's own release (20 Nov 2025, SRC_PALANTIR_IRAP_NOV25) says Palantir Platform Australia is "hosted in Australian Amazon Web Services (AWS) regions". It speaks for the platform, not for any one contract.
- 2026-10-22: the Hosting Certification Framework register archived in September (`data/raw/hcf/`) lists "Amazon Web Services - Cloud Service offering (Australian Regions)" among its certified cloud services. Palantir is not on it. The register certifies hosting providers, not the software that runs on them.
- 2026-10-22: the contract ledger (RG-086): 11 of the 29 Commonwealth notices name the US parent, Palantir Technologies Inc., including five of ASD's six. No notice says where a deployment runs.
- 2026-10-22: the FOI Act (compilation of 29 September 2026, archived at `data/raw/registers/legislation/`, sha256 `4f00f6ca`). Schedule 2 lists the Australian Signals Directorate as an exempt agency. Section 7(2A) makes any document that originated with ASD exempt in any agency's hands. Defence is exempt for documents on operational intelligence and special access programs. So the residency question can be put only to Defence, about Defence's own contracts.
- 2026-10-22: drafted FOI-002 to Defence (`docs/requests/FOI-002-defence-palantir-hosting.md`). It asks for the hosting and data-location terms of CN4104368, CN4115523 and CN4218984, framed around location and jurisdiction, not capability.

## Refused or restricted
- cyber.gov.au and defence.gov.au closed the connection without answering; nothing fetched.

## Leads (not facts)
- Search summaries say ASD retired its Certified Cloud Services List in 2020 and voided existing certifications, leaving IRAP assessments in their place. The cyber.gov.au page saying so did not answer the crawler.

## Outcome
security_records row 6 updated: AWS's Australian regions are certified and Palantir is not listed; ASD cannot be asked under the FOI Act. Per-deployment residency still needs FOI-002.
