The Hosting Certification Framework is currently undergoing reform.

The Department will pause the HCF Certification registration of prospective service providers and HCF Certified providers seeking supplementary assessment effective from 3 November 2025 until HCF reforms have been completed.

HCF Certified providers will not be impacted.
