Acoustic Assessment AirTrunk SYD2 Datacentre - 1 Sirius Rd, Lane 
Cove West - Peer Review 
Renzo Tonin & Associates (RTA) was engaged by EMKC Cubed Management (EMKC) to undertake an 
independent review of the Noise Impact Assessment (NIA) prepared by Pulse White Noise Acoustics 
(PWNA) for the Proposed S4.55 Modification Application for the AirTrunk SYD2 Datacentre, located at 1 
Sirius Road, Lane Cove West. 
The following documents prepared by PWNA were reviewed: 
• Acoustic Assessment AirTrunk Data Centre – 1 Sirius Road, Lane Cove (ref. 220364 – Acoustic 
Assessment Airtrunk Data Centre – 1 Sirius Road, Lane Cove – R14, dated 1 June 2023, Issue 15) 
• AirTrunk SYD2 – Operational Noise Monitoring (re. 220364-3 – AirTrunk SYD2 – Operational Noise 
Monitoring – R1, dated 24 March 2023, Final) 
• AirTrunk SYD2 – Response to S4.55 DA Modification, DPE Comments (re. 220364-5 – AirTrunk SYD2 
– Response to S4.55 DA Mod – R5, dated 2 May 2023) 
As part of RTA's review of PWNA's NIA, a range of items were identified as requiring further clarification, 
additional information and additional modelling, which led to several updates and revisions to the NIA. 
The peer review process did not involve any detailed modelling checks or remodelling by RTA. 
The peer review process involved RTA: 
• attending two online meetings with PWNA (one with the client and proponent attending as well), 
• partaking in three phone meetings with PWNA,  
• conducting seven reviews of the NIA, and 
• providing comments during each meeting and after each review of the NIA revisions.
