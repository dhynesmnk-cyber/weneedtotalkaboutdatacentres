1 Introduction 
This report provides the NSW Department of Planning, Industry and Environment’s (the Department’s) 
assessment of an application to modify the State significant development (SSD) consent for the Lane 
Cove West Data Centre (SSD-9741).  
The modification application seeks consent for the refurbishment and use of the existing office building 
at 2 Apollo Place as ancillary office space  to the approved data centre , and to undertake minor 
alterations and additions to improve the operational efficiency of the development.  
The application was lodged on 21 September 2020 by Greenbox Architecture Pty Ltd (the Applicant) 
pursuant to section 4.55(2) of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act). 
1.1 Background 
The Applicant is constructing a data centre at 1 Sirius Road, Lane Cove West in the Lane Cove local 
government area (see Figure 1).  
 
Figure 1 | Local context 
Data centres are places which are used by organisations to store their electronic computer applications 
and data. This could include your personal data (such as your Facebook account or Instagram), 
corporate data (such as a business’s payroll system) or sensitive government data (such as Medicare 
records).  

[[page 6]]
 
Lane Cove Data Centre Modification 2 (SSD-9741-Mod-2) | Modification Assessment Report 2 
In recent years, there has been an increased need for new large and small -scale data centres across 
the State. As new technologies (such as driverless cars, faster mobile  data networks and artificial 
intelligence) become commonplace, a significant number of large and small -scale data centres will be 
required to store the volume of electronic data accessed and created by Australians.  
1.2 Site description 
The site is located in the Lane Cove West Business Park, approximately 8 kilometres (km) north-west 
of the Sydney central business district (CBD), 13 km east of the Parramatta CBD and 3.5 km west of 
Chatswood. The site is legally described as Lot 1 in Deposited Plan (DP) 1151370  and has an area of 
approximately 3.9 hectares (ha).  
Two patches of the Plant Community Type (PCT) ‘Smooth-barked Apple - Red Bloodwood open forest’ 
are situated in the north-eastern and southern sections of the site. The north-western corner of the site 
was historically used for landfilling and is the subject of a current ‘Maintenance of Remediation Order’ 
(Notice No. 28027, dated 27/09/2005) issued by the Environment Protection Authority . 
The site is surrounded by industrial land uses and natural features (see Figure 2), including:  
• industrial and commercial buildings, such as Harley -Davidson Australia and New Zealand 
(1A Sirius Road) and the Lane Cove Tunnel Motorway Control Centre (5 Sirius Road) to the east 
• Lane Cove River, a large area of native vegetation and an Ausgrid-operated transmission line to 
the south and west 
• Stringybark Creek and the former ‘Cumberland Paper Mill’ site (now occupied by S. C. Johnson 
& Son) to the north. 
The nearest sensitive receivers are in ‘Arise Lane Cove’, a residential apartment tower located 
approximately 175 metres (m) to the north of the site (see Figure 1).

1.3 2 Apollo Place 
The Applicant has recently purchased an existing four-storey office building adjoining the south-eastern 
boundary of the site at 2 Apollo Place, Lane Cove West. The office building is legally described as Lot 7 
in DP 241877 and has an area of approximately 0.26 ha.  
The office building includes two levels of parking which are accessed from a driveway running parallel 
to the eastern side of the building . A separate driveway runs along the southern side of the building , 
servicing the adjacent industrial facility located at 3 Apollo Place. A small landscaped area is also 
located in front of the office building and comprises a mix of native and exotic plant species.  
The subject modification would involve the refurbishment and use of 2 Apollo Place as ancillary office 
space to the approved data centre (see Section 2 below). 
 
Figure 3 | Overview of 2 Apollo Place 
1.4 Approval history 
On 15 November 2019, development consent was granted by the then Executive Director, Compliance, 
Industry and Key Sites for the Lane Cove West Data Centre (SSD -9741). The development consent 
permits the c onstruction and 24 -hour operation of a data centre with associated office, supporting 
infrastructure and services, suspended driveway, car parking and landscaping.  
The development consent  was previously modified on 14 April 2020 (SSD -9741-Mod-1). This 
modification permitted the use of smaller, low voltage generators and included minor alterations and 
additions to improve the operational efficiency of the development. 
At the time of writing this report, the Applicant is progressing with construction of the suspended 
driveway, the on-site substation and the first phase of the data centre building.

2 Proposed modification 
On 21 September 2020, the Applicant lodged a modification application under section 4.55( 2) of the 
EP&A Act to modify development consent SSD -9741. The modification is described in full in the 
Statement of Environmental Effects (SEE) included in Appendix B and a comparison of the approved 
development against the proposed modification is illustrated in Figure 4 to Figure 7 below.  
The Applicant’s client intends to lease space within the development’s data halls to a single tenant, who 
will undertake the fit-out of this space with its own computer systems, server systems and networking 
equipment. 
The original SSD application (as modified) included a shared office space for on -site technicians 
employed by both the client and the tenant. These technicians would provide support to external users 
and undertake regular maintenance as necessary.  
Following further discussions with the tenant regarding its staffing and essential infrastructure 
requirements, the client has purchased the adjacent four-storey office building at 2  Apollo Place  to 
provide additional ancillary office space  for the site. The client has also identified a number of other 
minor alterations and additions to the data centre building to simplify construction arrangements and 
improve the operational efficiency of the development.  
Consequently, the Applicant proposes t he following changes to the approved development as part of 
the modification: 
• an increase in the development’s gross floor area (GFA), from 34,983 m2 to 39,319 m2 
• an increase in the number of operational jobs generated by the development , from 100 jobs to 
130 jobs 
• demolition of the southern section of the existing building at 2 Apollo Place to allow for the 
construction of a new driveway connecting Apollo Place and the approved data centre  
• refurbishment and use of the existing building at 2 Apollo Place as ancillary office space 
• construction of a link bridge between 2 Apollo Place and the data centre building  
• extension of the external plant platforms to provide sufficient clearance around plant equipment 
• amendments to the extent of the development’s western and southern fire trails 
• minor changes to landscaped areas to reflect the amended fire trails 
• minor amendments to the footprint of the substation 
• minor internal alterations and additions to the data centre building.

3 Strategic context 
3.1 Greater Sydney Region Plan 
The vision of the Greater Sydney Region Plan 2018, A Metropolis of Three Cities (the Region Plan) 
seeks to meet the needs of a growing and changing  population by transforming Greater Sydney into a 
metropolis of three cities – the Western Parkland City, the Central River City and the Eastern Harbour 
City. It brings new thinking to land  use and transport patterns to boost  Greater Sydney’s liveability, 
productivity and sustainability by spreading the benefits of growth. 
The proposed modification would continue to support the objectives of the Region Plan by:  
• providing additional, flexible  data storage capacity within the Eastern Economic Corridor 
(see Objective 3) 
• providing 30 additional, knowledge-intensive jobs closer to where people live (see Objective 23 
and Strategy 23.1). 
3.2 North District Plan 
The Greater Sydney Commission has released six district plans encompassing Greater Sydney, which 
will guide the delivery of the Region Plan. The subject site is located in the North District, which forms 
a large part of the Eastern Harbour City. The North District Plan (the NDP) is a 20-year plan to manage 
economic, social and  environmental matters in northern Sydney. It is a guide for implementing the 
Region Plan at a district level and is a bridge between regional and local planning. 
The proposed modification would continue to align with the actions of the NDP by:  
• supporting Sydney’s status as a global city and ensuring knowledge -intensive jobs are located 
close to areas of high population growth (see actions 36 to 39) 
• continuing to support the retention and improvement of industrial-zoned land within the Lane 
Cove West Precinct (see actions 46 to 49). 
The Department has considered the strategic context of the site and is satisfied the proposed 
modification is consistent with the intent of the relevant strategic planning documents.

4 Statutory context 
4.1 Scope of modifications 
The Department has reviewed the scope of the modification application and considers that the 
application can be characterised as a modification as it:  
• would not significantly increase the environmental impacts of the development as approved 
• is substantially the same development as originally approved 
• is of a scale that warrants the use of section 4.55(2) of the EP&A Act. 
The Department has considered the scale of the proposed changes, including the refurbish ment and 
use of the existing office building at 2 Apollo Place (see Section 6 of this report), and is satisfied the 
proposed modification is within the scope of section 4.55(2) of the EP&A Act and does not constitute a 
new development application.  
Accordingly, the Department considers that the application should be assessed and determined under 
section 4.55(2) of the EP&A Act rather than requiring a new development application to be lodged.  
4.2 Consent authority 
The Minister for Planning  and Public Spaces ( the Minister) is the consent authority for the application 
under section 4.5( a) of the EP&A Act. Under the Minister ’s delegation of 9 March 2020, the Director, 
Industry Assessments, may determine the application under delegation as:  
• the application has not already been referred by the Planning Secretary to the Independent 
Planning Commission as at the date of the delegation 
• a political disclosure statement has not been made 
• there are less than ten public submissions in the nature of objections.  
4.3 Mandatory matters for consideration 
The Department undertook a comprehensive assessment of the development against the mandatory 
matters for consideration as part of its original assessment of SSD -9741. The Department considers 
this modification application does not result in significant changes that would alter the mandatory 
matters for consideration under section 4.15 of the EP&A Act and the conclusions made as part of the 
original assessment. 
4.4 Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016 
Section 7.17 of the Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016  (the BC Act) specifies that a Biodiversity 
Development Assessment Report (BDAR) must be sub mitted and taken into consideration for any 
proposed modification to an SSD consent, unless the determining authority is satisfied the modification 
will not increase the impact of the development upon biodiversity values.  
The Department’s assessment of the Applicant’s updated BDAR , including any additional impacts to 
biodiversity values associated with the modification application, is provided in Section 6 of this report.

5 Engagement 
5.1 Department’s engagement 
In accordance with clause 10 of Schedule 1 to the EP&A Act  and clause 118 of the Environmental 
Planning and Assessment Regulation 2000 (the EP&A Regulation), the Department: 
• made the modification application publicly available from 28 September 2020 until 12 October 
2020 (15 days) on the Major Projects website 
• notified and invited comment from those who made a submission on the original development 
application by letter 
• notified and invited comment from relevant public authorities and Lane Cove Council (Council) 
by letter. 
A total of seven submissions were received on the modification application during the exhibition period, 
including: 
• three from public authorities 
• one from Council 
• one from a special interest group 
• two from the public.  
Of the seven submissions received, one objected to the modification application and six provided 
comments. A summary of the issues raised in submissions is provided below, with a copy of each 
submission included in Appendix B.  
5.2 Key issues – Council and the relevant public authorities 
During the exhibition period, the Department received four submissions from Council and the relevant 
public authorities. None of the public authorities have objected to the development, and the key issues 
raised in their submissions have  been addressed through the Applicant’s Response to Submissions 
(RTS) or through the recommended conditions of consent. The submissions received are summarised 
as follows: 
Council recommended the Department carefully consider whether the modification can be considered 
as ‘substantially the same development’ as approved under SSD -9741, given the addition of 3,053 m 2 
of new land is significant. Council also sought clarification as to whether any additional trees would be 
removed to accommodate the larger fire trails and plant platforms and , if so, recommended the trees 
be replaced at a ratio of 2:1. 
The Environment, Energy and Science Group  (EESG) requested the development’s BDAR  be 
updated to assess potential impacts associated with the works proposed at 2 Apollo Place, and sought 
clarification regarding whether any additional landscaping/revegetation  works were proposed  at this 
site. 
Transport for NSW  (TfNSW) recommended the developme nt’s Travel Access Guide (TAG) and 
Sustainable Travel and Access Plan (STAP) be updated to incorporate 2 Apollo Place.

Biodiversity 
The proposed amendments to the fire trails and plant platforms have the 
potential to result in additional impacts to existing on -site trees and the

7 Evaluation

capacity within the Eastern Economic Corridor 
• not significantly increase the environmental impacts of the development beyond those assessed 
under the original development application.
