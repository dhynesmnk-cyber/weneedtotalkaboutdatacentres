2.3  
 Uses and Activities  
The primary function of the development is to facilitate the 
 secure collection, storage, processing and 
distribution of electronic 
 data through a purpose
 -
built data centre campus comprising 3 data centre 
buildings (DC01, DC02 and DC03). The facility would have a total IT capacity of approximately 
193.6  
MW 
and operate on a 24
 -
hour per day, 7
 -
day per week basis.  
The data centre buildings would contain data halls, electrical infrastructure, plant and equipment, and 
ancillary  
office and administration areas. The facility would be managed by facilities management and 
security teams responsible for monitoring, maintaining and operating the services required to support 
customer IT infrastructure. 
 On
 -
site security personnel would provide monitoring, patrol, and access 
functions.  
The development would utilise a direct evaporative cooling system to maintain operating conditions 
within the data halls. 
 E
 ach data hall 
 would be  
 served by dedicated 
 Data Hall Air Handling Units  
(DAHUs).  
The facility would be supplied by 3 internal substations and associated electrical infrastructure. I
 n 
the event of a power outage, uninterrupted power would be provided through a backup power system 
comprising 97  
 diesel generators and lithium
 -
ion battery systems. The batteries provide temporary 
power while the generators start and assume the
