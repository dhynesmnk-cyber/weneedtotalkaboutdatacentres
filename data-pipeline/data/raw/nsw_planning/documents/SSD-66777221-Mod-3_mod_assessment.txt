Preface 
This assessment report provides a record of the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure’s 
(the Department) assessment and evaluation of Goodman Property Services (Aust) Ptd Limited 
application to modify State si gnificant development (SSD) consent for the Lanceley Place Data 
Centre, located in the Willoughby local government area. This report includes:  
• a description of the proposed modification 
• an assessment of the modification against government policy and statutory requirements, 
including mandatory considerations 
• a demonstration of how matters raised by government agencies have been considered 
• an assessment of the likely environmental, social and economic impacts of the modification 
• an evaluation which weighs up the likely impacts and benefits of the modification, having 
regard to the proposed mitigation measures, and provides a view on whether the impacts are, 
on balance, acceptable 
• a recommendation to the decision -maker, along with the reasons for the recommendation, to 
assist them in making an informed decision about whether the approval should be modified and 
any conditions that should be imposed.

1 Introduction 
The modification application seeks to  modify the development consent for the Lanceley Place Data 
Centre (SSD- 66777221-Mod-3) to facilitate architectural modifications to the switch room and 
reinstatement of an existing fire brigade crossover.  
The application was lodged on 2 June 2026  by Goodman Property Services (Aust) Pty Limited (the 
Applicant), pursuant to section 4.55(1A) of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A 
Act). 
A more detailed overview of the proposed modification is provided in Section 2 of this report. 
1.1 Background 
The Applicant has approval to construct a data centre at 2-8 Lanceley Place and 14 Campbell Street, 
Artarmon in the Willoughby local government area (LGA) (see Figure 1 ).

2 Proposed Modification 
2.1 Modification Overview 
The modification is described in full in the Modification Report included in Appendix A , summarised 
in Table 2  and is illustrated in Figure 3  below.

the site's northern frontage to Campbell Street to accommodate the selected gas -insulated 
switchgear and required maintenance clearances, and the reinstatement of an existing crossover on 
Lanceley Place to maintain Fire and Rescue NSW access to the fire booster assembly. 
The modification also includes updates to the approved architectural and landscape plans to reflect 
the proposed changes.

2.2 Applicant’s Justification for the Proposed Modification 
The Applicant advises that the proposed amendments arise from  the ongoing detailed design 
development of the project and the refinement of infrastructure requirements. The changes are 
intended to support the delivery, operation and maintenance of the site's electrical infrastructure and 
fire safety systems while maintaining consistency with the approved development.  This includes a 
minor increase in the height of the switch room to accommodate the selected gas -insulated 
switchgear and associated maintenance clearances. In addition, as identified in the approved Fire 
Safety Study, Fire and Rescue NSW has requested tha t t he existi ng c rossover ad ja ce nt to t h e fire 
booster assembly be retained to ensure safe and efficient access for inspection, maintenance and 
emergency response purposes.

3.2 Mandatory Matters for Consideration 
The Department undertook a comprehensive assessment of the application against the mandatory 
matters for consideration as part of the original assessment of SSD -66777221 (section 4.15 of the 
EP&A Act). This modification application does not result  in significant changes that would alter the 
Department’s consideration of the mandatory matters for consideration under section 4.15(1) of the 
EP&A Act and conclusions made as part of the original assessment. 
3.3 Biodiversity Development Assessment Report  
Section 7.17(2) of the Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016 (BC Act) requires all SSD modifications to be 
accompanied by a Biodiversity Development Assessment Report (BDAR) unless the authority or 
person determining the application is satisfied that the modification will not increase the impact on 
biodiversity values (as identified in the BC Act and in the Biodiversity Conservation Regulation 2017) 
The Department is satisfied that the modification will not increase the impact on biodiversity values 
as the proposed changes are largely confirmed to existing, disturbed areas previously assessed for 
biodiversity values under the original approval.  
The modification will not require the removal of any additional trees beyond what was considered 
under the original assessment and, consequently, a BDAR is not required to accompany the 
modification application.

4.1 Department’s Engagement 
In accordance with the Environmental Planning and Assessment Regulation 2021 ( EP&A Regulation) 
the Department made the modification application publicly available on the Department’s website on 
16 June 2026 and forwarded the application to Willoughby City Council (Council) and Fire and Rescue 
NSW (FRNSW) for comment.

The Department received advice from Council and FRNSW on the modification application. A summary 
of the advice is provided in Table 4. A link to the full copy of the advice is provided in Appendix B .

Authority Advice Summary 
Council  Council provided comments on the modification and advised that the submitted documentation 
does not demonstrate whether FRNSW has specified design requirements for the proposed fire 
brigade crossover. Council recommended that the Applicant consult with FRNSW regarding 
emergency vehicle access design requirements and proposed a condition requiring detailed 
driveway design information to be provided at the Construction Certificate Stage to ensure safe 
vehicular access.

6 Evaluation 
The Department’s assessment has considered the relevant matters and objects of the EP&A Act and 
considers the proposed modification is appropriate on the basis that: 
• the proposed modification will not result in any material environmental impacts beyond those 
previously assessed and approved 
• the modification would facilitate a number of minor design refinements to supporting site 
infrastructure as a result of the detailed design process of the development 
Overall, the Department considers the impacts of the modification would be minor and acceptable 
and concludes it can be approved, subject to the recommended, modified conditions.
