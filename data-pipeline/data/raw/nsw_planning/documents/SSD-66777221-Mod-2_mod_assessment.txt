Preface 
This assessment report provides a record of the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure’s 
(the Department’s) assessment and evaluation of modification 2 to the State significant development 
(SSD) consent for the Lanceley  Place Data Centre , located at 2 -8 Lanceley Place and 14 Campbell 
Street, Artarmon, and lodged by Goodman Property Services (Aust) Pty Limited. The report includes: 
• a description of the proposed modification 
• an assessment of the modification against government policy and statutory requirements, 
including mandatory considerations 
• a demonstration of how matters raised by the community and other stakeholders have  been 
considered 
• an assessment of the likely environmental, social and economic impacts of the modification 
• an evaluation which weighs up the likely impacts and benefits of the modification, having regard 
to the proposed mitigation  measures, offsets, community views and government/expert advice, 
and provides a view on whether the impacts are, on balance, acceptable 
• a recommendation to the decision -maker, along with the reasons for the recommendation, to 
assist them in making an informed decision about whether the consent should be modified and 
any conditions that should be imposed.

1 Introduction 
The modification application seeks to amend the development consent for the Lanceley Place Data 
Centre (SSD-66777221) to implement minor changes, including a reduction in floor space ratio, design 
amendments to the rooftop layout, external materiality and appearance, and deep soil and 
landscaping amendments. Other amendments include infrastructure delivery changes , and various 
modifications to the back-up generator and stack arrangements.  
The application was lodged on 27 January 2026 by Goodman Property Services (Aust) Pty Limited (the 
Applicant), pursuant to section 4.55(1A) of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 
(EP&A Act). 
A more detailed overview of the proposed modification as amended is provided in Section 2 below. 
1.1 Background 
The Applicant has approval to construct a data centre at 2-8 Lanceley Place and 14 Campbell Street, 
Artarmon in the Willoughby local government area  (LGA) and within the Northern Sydney region of 
NSW (see Figure 1). 
1.2 Subject Site 
The site is legally described as Lot 11 DP 606737 and Lots 12 -15 DP 233037 and known as 
2-8 Lanceley Place and 14 Campbell Street, Artarmon. The site is located immediately east of the 
Gore Hill Technology Park. The closest residential area and a hotel are located approximately 
200 metres (m) west of the site, across the Pacific Highway. North Shore Hospital and Royal North 
Shore Hospital are located approximately 170 m and 390 m southeast of the site, respectively. TAFE 
NSW’s St Leonards campus is situated about 180 m to the southwest (see Figure 2).

2.2 Applicant’s Justification for the Proposed Modification 
The Applicant has advised the proposed modification will not significantly affect the suitability of 
the site and the objectives of the development as approved under SSD-66777221. The proposed 
amendments seek to maintain the development’s compliance with the applicable planning controls. 
The proposed amendments generally relate to ongoing detailed design development and minor 
changes made to the Applicant’s operational requirements, to help ensure an efficient design is 
constructed on the site. The changes seek to enhance the communal rooftop layout, elevate 
external materiality and appearance, refine deep soil and landscaping works, update the delivery 
infrastructure, and improve the operational efficiency of the generators and their associated stacks.

Section 7.17(2) of the Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016 (BC Act) requires all SSD modifications to be 
accompanied by a Biodiversity Development Assessment Report (BDAR) unless the authority or 
person determining the application is satisfied that the modification will not increase the impact on 
biodiversity values (as identified in the BC Act and in the Biodiversity Conservation Regulation 2017)  
The Department is satisfied that the modification will not increase the impact on biodiversity values 
and consequently a BDAR is not required to accompany the modification application.

6 Evaluation 
The Department’s assessment has considered the relevant matters and objects of the EP&A Act  and 
considers the proposed modification is appropriate on the basis that: 
• the proposed modification will result in minimal environmental impacts beyond the 
approved facility  
• the proposal will continue to facilitate the orderly and economic use and development of 
the land 
• the proposal will continue to provide construction and operational jobs. 
The modified development is relatively similar in size and scale to the approved development, and the 
façade design changes are considered acceptable.  
While the modification will result in a small increase in noise impacts during the daytime period, it will 
not increase evening or night -time impacts, and the operation of the development (as modified)  will 
continue to comply with the requirements of the NPfI. Consequently, the Department is satisfied that 
these impacts are acceptable in the context of the site. 
The Department is satisfied the modification should be approved subject to conditions.
