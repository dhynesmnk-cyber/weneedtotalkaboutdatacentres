DigiCo SYD1 Data Centre Expansion - 400 Harris Street, Ultimo - 
Peer review of Noise and Vibration Impact Assessment  
Renzo Tonin & Associates (RTA) was engaged by Patch Planning, on behalf of HDI SYD1 Property 
Holdings Pty Limited (DigiCo) to peer review the noise and vibration impact assessment (NVIA) report 
undertaken by Pulse White Noise Acoustics (PWNA) which was prepared to support the State Significant 
Development Application (SSDA) 69637456 for the proposed DigiCo Sydney data centre expansion 
project, located at 390-422 Harris Street and 273 Pyrmont Street, Ultimo (the Project). 
The following documents prepared by PWNA were reviewed: 
 HDI SYD1 Data Centre – State Significant Development Application (SSDA) Noise and Vibration 
Impact Assessment (ref. 240218 – HDI SYD1 Data Centre – State Significant Development 
Application (SSDA) Noise and Vibration Impact Assessment – R3, dated 4 April 2025).  
 Updated revisions from Revision 4 to Revision 10 were reviewed with comments provided.  
As part of RTA's review of PWNA's NVIA, a range of items were identified as requiring further 
clarification, additional noise measurements, additional and updated information, tables and figures, 
revised project noise trigger levels, and revised noise modelling, which led to several updates and 
revisions to the NVIA. The peer review process did not involve any detailed modelling checks or 
remodelling by RTA. 
RTA’s peer review process involved: 
 attending three online meetings with PWNA and project team and multiple phone meetings, 
 conducting reviews or partial reviews of eight revisions of the NVIA, and 
 providing comments during each meeting and following reviews of the NVIA revisions.
