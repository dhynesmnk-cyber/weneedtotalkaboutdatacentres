2.2  
 Applicant’s Justification for the Proposed Modification  
The Applicant advises that the operational parameters and technologies associated with back
 -
up 
diesel generators have evolved since the approval of SSD ‑
10330 ‑
Mod ‑
1. Ongoing investigations by 
both the Applicant and the operational tenant have identified updated requirements for generator 
capacity, testing regimes and system performance to ensure reliable emergency power supply to the 
data centre.  
The proposed modification responds to these operational findings. The Applicant submits that 
increasing the number of diesel backup generators is necessary to maintain uninterrupted power 
during a mains outage, thereby ensuring that operational tenants ret
 ain continuous access to their 
data and minimising any risk of service downtime.
