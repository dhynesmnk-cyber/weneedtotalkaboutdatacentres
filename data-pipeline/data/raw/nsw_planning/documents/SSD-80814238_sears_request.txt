Describe the permissibility of the proposal under relevant environmental planning instruments
The portion of the site relevant to this SEARs request is zoned E3 (Productivity Support) under the Ryde LEP 2014. Under the Ryde Local Environmental Plan 2014, data centres,
which are a type of ‘high technology industry’, which in turn is a type of ‘light industry’ are permissible with consent on E3 (Productivity Support) zoned land.
