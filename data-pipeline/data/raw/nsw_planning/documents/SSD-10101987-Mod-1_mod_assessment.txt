1 Introduction 
The modification application seeks to  modify the development consent for the Kemps Creek Data 
Centre (SSD-10101987) to permit fit-
 . 
The application was lodged on 21 October 2024  by ARUP Pty Limited (the Applicant), pursuant to 
section 4.55(1A) of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act). 
A more detailed overview of the proposed modification is provided in Section 2 of this report. 
1.1 Background 
The Applicant is currently constructing at data centre at The Yards industrial estate (SSD -9522), 
which forms part of the Mamre Road Precinct within the Penrith local government area (see Figure 1).  
The Applicant is a global advisory, planning, engineering, and architectural firm that specializes in the 
design and delivery of large-scale projects across the world, including data centre projects.  
The Applicant has been approached by a data centre operator (the end user) who is seeking to expand 
its operations within the Sydney region and, in particular, western Sydney. The development is being 
facilitated by the Applicant and will be operated by the end user.  
At the time of writing this report, the Applicant has largely completed construction of the first data 
hall building, with operations likely to commence in late 2024 / early 2025.  The subject modification 
is being sought by the Applicant to allow for fit-out works to occur prior to operations commencing at 
the site.  
1.2  Subject site 
The site was described as lots X & Y in DP 421633 and Lot 22  in DP 258414 within the original State 
significant development (SSD) application. Following recent subdivision works to facilitate delivery of 
the Yards Estate, the subject site is now legally descr ibed as Lot 2 in DP 1293204 and is located off 
Buda Way in Kemps Creek.  
The subject site is located approximately 40 kilometres (km) west of the Sydney CBD, 22 km west of 
Parramatta and 12 km south-east of Penrith within the Mamre Road Precinct (see Figure 1).   
The site is a 14.43 hectares (ha) parcel of land located in the south-west portion of The Yards industrial 
estate (see Figure 2). Access to the  industrial estate (including the development ) is provided via a 
signalised intersection off Mamre Road and Bakers Lane, which was constructed under the SSD-9522 
consent.  
The nearest residential receivers to the site are located approximately 700 metres (m) to the west, 
across Wianamatta-South Creek, in the residential area known as Luddenham (Twin Creeks Estate). 
Several schools and a retirement village are also located to the east of the site (see Figure 2).

2 Proposed Modification 
2.1 Modification overview 
The proposed modification is described in full in the modification report included in Appendix A . The 
modification application seeks to amend the development co nsent to allow for the installation of 
computer equipment within the 
 The computing equipment would primarily 
consist of the end-
 -populated server systems and their associated storage racks.  
An example of server racking typically installed within data centres is shown in Figure 4 below.  
 
Figure 4 | Example of data hall server racking 
2.2 
 justification for the modification 
The Applicant advised that, ordinarily, such fit -out works would be undertaken as complying 
development under Part 5 of State Environmental Planning Policy (Exempt and Complying 
Development Codes) 2008, as this arrangement would allow for the server equipment and associated 
racking systems to be easily upgraded over the life of the development.  
However, under clause 2.35 of State Environmental Planning Policy (Industry and Employment) 2021, 
the overall cost of the fit-out works would trigger the need for concurrence from Transport for NSW 
and, accordingly, prevents the Applicant from utilising the complying development pathway.  
Consequently, the Applicant is seeking to amend the consent to allow for the fit -out works to be 
carried out, in order to ensure operation of the development can commence in a timely manner.   
The Applicant also highlighted the exclusion of references to the  fit-out works in the original 
Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) was an error, and noted the 
 supporting technical reports 
fully considered all potential environmental impacts associated with the fit-out works.

3.2 Mandatory matters for consideration 
The Department undertook a comprehensive assessment of the application against the mandatory 
matters for consideration as part of the original assessment of SSD-10101987 (under section 4.15 of 
the EP&A Act). This modification application does not result in significant chang es that would alter 
 matters for consideration under section 4.15(1) of 
the EP&A Act and conclusions made as part of the original assessment. 
3.3 Biodiversity development assessment report  
Section 7.17(2) of the Biodiversity Conservation Act 2016 (BC Act) requires all SSD modifications to be 
accompanied by a Biodiversity Development Assessment Report (BDAR) unless the authority or 
person determining the application is satisfied that the modification will not increase the impact on 
biodiversity values (as identified in the BC Act and in the Biodiversity Conservation Regulation 2017) 
The Department is satisfied that the modification will not increase the impact on biodiversity values , 
as the fit -out works are internal  and would be limited to the footprint of the approved data hall 
buildings. Accordingly, a BDAR is not required to accompany the modification application.

5 Assessment 
The Department has assessed the merits of the proposed modification. During this assessment, the 
Department has considered the: 
• modification report provided to support the proposed modification (see Appendix A ) 
• documentation relating to the original SSD application (see Appendix A ) 
• advice received from Council (see Appendix A )  
• relevant environmental planning instruments, policies and guidelines 
• requirements of the EP&A Act, including the objects of the EP&A Act. 
The proposed modification seeks  approval for the installation of computer equipment within the 
  
In the modification report, the Applicant noted the original EIS and associated technical reports 
considered all aspects of the development during construction and operation, including those impacts 
associated with the installation and use of computing equipment within the data halls.  
During the notification period, Council advised it had no objections in relation to the proposed 
modification, provided the development continues to comply with relevant Australian Standards, 
particularly in relation to fire safety and access.  
The Department has carefully considered the proposed fit-out works in the context of the approved 
development and is satisfied that they are minor and acceptable, particularly as:  
• references to the fit-out works were excluded from the original SSD application by mistake  
• all construction and operation-related impacts associated with th e computing equipment have 
previously been considered and assessed as part of the SSD application.  
The Department has recommended the updated architectural plans included in the modification 
report be incorporated into the approved development consent. The Departm ent considers the se 
plans are suitably flexible and will allow for the server systems and their associated storage racks to 
be periodically upgraded over the life of the development , without triggering the need for further 
modification applications.
