1.0 Introduction  
This Modification Application Report has been prepared by Colliers Urban Planning on behalf of Stockland Fife Kemps 
Creek Pty Ltd (SFKC) to accompany a Modification Application pursuant to Section 4.55(1A) of the Environmental Planning 
and Assessment Act 1979 (EP&A Act). It seeks to modify State Significant Development (SSD) 10479 relating to the 
approved 200 Aldington Road Industrial Estate, Kemps Creek (The Project). This Modification Application is submitted to 
the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure (DPHI). 
This Modification Application relates to the approved Aldington Road upgrade component of the Concept and Stage 1 
development and seeks to amend the configuration of Intersection 4. The purpose of the modification is to amend the 
footprint of the interim and ultimate roadworks design of the intersection. The amended road design will locate the full 
width local road on the west of Intersection 4 to be entirely within 99–111 Aldington Road (Lot 37 in DP258949), as 
opposed to the previously approved footprint which provided only part of the local road on that property with the 
remainder of the local road being positioned within the adjoining property at 53–97 Aldington Road (Lot 38 in DP708347). 
The Modification also seeks to amend the timing of the Interim Roadworks (Condition F5) to permit the operation of the 
approved development on Lots F, K and L upon the installation of the interim traffic signals at the Mamre Road / Abbotts 
Road Intersection and the completion of the first stage of the Aldington Road Widening Works, including Intersection 3.   
The Modification Application is based on the amended Civil Engineering Plans provided by AT&L (see Appendix B) and 
traffic assessments provided by Ason (see Appendix D, Appendix E and Appendix M) and other supporting technical 
information appended to the Report (see Table of Contents). This Report has been prepared in accordance with Part 5 of 
the EP&A Regulations and DPHI’s State Significance Development Guidelines – Preparing a Modification Report. It identifies 
the consent to be modified, describes the proposed modifications and provides a planning assessment of the relevant 
matters for consideration.

1.2 Consent Proposed to be Modified

Significant Concept Plan and Stage 1 Development Application for the 200 Aldington Road Industrial Estate, comprising a

for the (then) consolidated Lot H into Lot F and Lot E (similar to what was approved under MOD 1). It also amended 
the Stage 1 component by relocating the approved warehouse from Lot K to Lot F.

original consent to include road widening and upgrades to Aldington and Abbotts Road, and the upgrade of the 
Mamre Road and Abbotts Road intersection.

2.0 Locational and strategic context  
2.1 Site Context  
The 200 Aldington Road Industrial Estate is located within the Mamre Road Precinct (MRP) which forms part of the wider 
Western Sydney Employment Area (WSEA). It is located approximately 9km north-east of the future Western Sydney 
International (WSI) Airport due to open in 2026, 15km south-east of the Penrith CBD and 37km west of the Sydney CBD. 
 An aerial context map is provided in Figure 2.

2.2 Strategic context  
The approved development’s (as proposed to be modified) alignment with its strategic context remains consistent with 
the approved Project. In summary, the modified development remains consistent with: 
• The Greater Sydney Regional Plan in that the development is essential to support the freight and logistics network in 
the Western Parkland City and would utilise industrial zoned land for warehouse and logistics uses.  
• The Western City District Plan in that it supports the wider industrial area and would maximise freight and logistic 
opportunities, and planning for and managing industrial and urban services land, as well as supporting the growing 
investment, business opportunities and jobs in strategic centres.  
• The Mamre Road Precinct in that it is consistent with the Structure Plan and delivers a development in an orderly 
and coordinated manner.  
• The Western Sydney Aerotropolis Plan in that it provides for opportunities for logistics and distribution land uses to 
connect Western Sydney to the broader freight network and supports the future operations of the airport.

Condition F4 – Notification of Commencement  
The date of commencement of each of the following phases of the Interim Road Works must be notified to the Planning Secretary

Condition F5 – Timing  
F5. The Interim Road Works must be constructed, to the satisfaction of the relevant roads authorities, as follows 
(a) the first stage of the Aldington Road Widening Works, including intersection 3 works, prior to operation of the first 
warehouse on the Site, as shown on Figure 6 in Appendix 6; 
(b) the second stage of the Aldington Road Widening Works, including intersection 4 as shown in the 3000-series civil

(a) the second stage of the Aldington Road Widening Works, including intersection 4 as shown in the 3000-series civil 
works package, prior to the operation of any warehouses other than on Lots F, J and K; 
(b) the first stage of the Aldington Road Widening Works, including intersection 3 works, prior to operation of 
warehouses on Lots F, J and K, as shown on Figure 6 in Appendix 6; 
(c) Abbotts Road Widening Works, prior to operation of the first warehouse on the Site, as shown in red shading on Figure 6 in 
Appendix 6; and 
(c1) the Mamre Road and Abbotts Road Intersection ‘Interim Traffic Signals’ to the satisfaction of the relevant road 
authorities prior to the operation of Lots F, J and K; and, 
(d) the complete Mamre Road and Abbotts Road Intersection Works, including the operation of interim traffic signals, as 
described in the 2000 series civil works package and shown in orange shading on Figure 6 in Appendix 6, prior to operation of 
any warehouse not referred to in Condition F5(c1). 
Rationale: The conditions are required to be updated to ensure that warehouses on Lots F, J and K (which are approved 
and currently under construction) could commence operation: 
• Upon the operation of the interim traffic signals at the Mamre Road / Abbotts Road intersection; and,  
• Prior to the completion of the Stage 2 roadworks (Intersection 4), which will be delayed due to the required revisions 
to Intersection 4 addressed in this modification. 
Practically, heavy vehicles operating at 200 Aldington Road cannot travel north through Intersection 4 via Bakers Lane 
under the consent and the internal road network on the estate provides a compliant exit from the estate to Aldington 
Road via Intersection 3. The Applicant has prepared an Operational Traffic Management Plan (OTMP) (refer to Appendix 
M) which includes operation of Lots F, J and K via Intersection 3 and to ensure operational traffic with the associated 
buildings does not impact road safety and network efficiency noting the staged delivery of the Road Network.

Condition F5A (New Condition) – Timing  
Prior to the commencement of operation of Lot F, J and K, the Applicant must prepare an Operational Traffic 
Management Plan (OTMP). The OTMP must:  
(a) be prepared by a suitably qualified and experienced person(s) 
(b) detail the measures to be implemented during the period the Interim Traffic Signals are in place to manage 
operational traffic with concurrent construction traffic from the site and the External Road Works and other public 
traffic, to ensure road safety and network efficiency at all times; 
(c) detail heavy vehicle routes, turning restrictions, access and parking arrangements; 
(d) include an Operational Driver Code of Conduct to: 
(i) minimise impacts on the local and regional road network; 
(ii) minimise conflicts with other road users; 
(iii) minimise road traffic noise; 
(iv) inform truck drivers of the site access arrangements, turning restrictions and use of specified 
routes; 
(v) include a program to monitor the effectiveness of these measures; and 
(vi) detail the compliance actions that would be implemented for any vehicles that deviate from approved 
routes and turning restrictions. 
Rationale: To require the preparation of a OTMP to enable operation of lots F, J and K in advance of the Interim Road 
Works being completed, generally consistent with the requirement for other warehouses within the Aldington Road 
corridor such as Westlink Stage 1 (SSD-9138102 MOD 7).

the development to which the consent as modified relates is substantially the same development as the development for which 
the consent was originally granted and before that consent as originally granted was modified (if at all)”.

• The proposed design remains generally consistent with the approved development and only modified to 
accommodate the realignment of intersection 4 to locate the local road fully within the 99-111 Aldington Road site.  
• There are no physical changes proposed to the approved warehouse-built form.  
• The envisioned uses on the site remain unchanged being industrial, warehouse and distribution uses; 
• The public benefit delivered by the development remains unchanged; 
• The modification does not alter the approved development’s level of compliance with the applicable environmental 
planning instruments and policies, including its strategic context, as presented in Section 2.0; 
• As demonstrated in Section 6.0, the changes will not result in any significant new environmental impacts from those 
considered and addressed as part of the original development; and

same development for which consent was originally granted.

6.1 Biodiversity  
An Ecological Assessment Addendum letter has been prepared by Fraser Ecological and is provided in Appendix C. The 
addendum letter confirms that the following additional works are required as a result of the proposed modification:  
• Additional earthworks beyond the previously approved extent of roadworks;  
• Modification of an existing farm dam south of the road alignment; and  
• Construction of a bund to manage stormwater following road construction.  
The additional land associated with the proposed modification is identified as ‘Certified – urban capable land’ under the 
Cumberland Plain Conservation Plan.  
The addendum letter confirms that additional impact footprint to the west was found to comprise introduced 
environmental pasture grass species of low ecological significance, with no threatened flora, fauna, or ecological 
communities identified within the additional works area. Notably, the expanded footprint does require the dewatering of 
an existing farm dam, which is dominated by environmental weed species and commonly occurring native species such 
as Typha orientalis and Persicaria deciphens. The existing dam is indicated by a red arrow within Figure 6 below.  
Therefore, Fraser Ecological have confirmed that the proposed amendments do not increase impact to threatened 
species previously assessed, nor does it increase any biodiversity or species credit requirements. No change is proposed 
to the approved aquatic ecology mitigation measures, which address appropriate dewatering procedures for several 
existing farm dams throughout the project.

the set distance of the road works in each allotment throughout the Aldington Road Precinct. Ultimately, the ‘clearly

receiver R02. This represents an insignificant change and does not necessitate additional noise modelling.

consistent with those previously assessed in the CNVIA.
