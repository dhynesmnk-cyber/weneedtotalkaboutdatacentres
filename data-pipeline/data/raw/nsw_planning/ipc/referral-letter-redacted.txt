Dear Professor O’Kane 
The Department has completed its assessment of the development application for an expansion to an 
existing data centre located at 17 -23 Talavera Road, Macquarie Park within the City of Ryde local 
government area.  
In accordance with clause 8A of the former State Environmental Planning Policy (State and Regional 
Development) 2011 and section 4.5 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 , the 
Independent Planning Commission is the declared consent authority for this development, as:  
• City of Ryde Council has objected to the development  
• a company connected to Macquarie Data Centres Pty Ltd (the Applicant) has made reportable 
political donations in the two-year period prior to the application being made. 
Based on its detailed assessment, the Department is satisfied the development is approvable, subject 
to the recommended conditions of consent.  
I have attached a copy of the Department’s assessment report and the recommended instrument of 
consent for the Commission’s consideration.  
Further information regarding the development can be found on the NSW planning portal at:  
https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/talavera-road-data-centre-campus-
expansion.
