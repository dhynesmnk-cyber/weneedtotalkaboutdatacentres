My investment options
CBUS investment holdings
We invest in many different types of assets on behalf of our members
Our ‘Pre-mixed’ investment options allow members to invest in these different assets. Members can select which of our investment options best suit their risk appetite and retirement goals.
The different assets in which we invest include Cbus Property developments, infrastructure assets like ports and toll roads, shares in listed and private Australian and international companies, cash, debt and fixed income.
We are required to publish where each investment option invests for 31 December and 30 June every year across super, transition to retirement and retirement.
The way information is shown is set by regulations. Some investments will have an individual value and percentage, some will be grouped with a total investment and percentage amount shown. Values displayed are for each investment option at the reporting date.
Depending on the investment option you’re looking at you will see a higher, lower or no investment across the different asset classes, for example the Cash investment option does not invest in other asset classes.
The information published below is for 30 June 2026.
