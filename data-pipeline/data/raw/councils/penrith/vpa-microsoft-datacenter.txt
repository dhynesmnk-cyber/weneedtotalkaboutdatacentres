Voluntary Planning Agreement - Microsoft Datacenter
Listen
Penrith City Council publicly notified a Voluntary Planning Agreement (VPA) between Council, and Microsoft Datacenter (Australia) Pty Ltd. The site relating to this VPA is within the approved industrial estate “The Yards” located at 657-769 Mamre Road, Kemps Creek. Specifically, the VPA applies to 769 Mamre Road, Kemps Creek, known as Lot 2 DP 1271142.
At Council's Ordinary Meeting of 29 May 2023, Council endorsed the VPA for execution. The VPA was executed on 7 June 2023.
The purpose of the VPA is to collect monetary contributions for the future development of a data center located at the abovementioned address. The monetary contribution will support the delivery of infrastructure including open space and landscaping embellishment within the Mamre Road Precinct.
A copy of the executed VPA and explanatory note can be viewed online at yoursaypenrith.com.au
The notification period was from Thursday 9 March 2023 to Thursday 6 April 2023.
Please contact Natalie White, Planner, on (02) 4732 7833 or email  natalie.white@penrith.city for enquiries.
