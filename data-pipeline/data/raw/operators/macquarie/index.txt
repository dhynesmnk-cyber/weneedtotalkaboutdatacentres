Our data centres.
Our data centres are trusted by hyperscalers, multinational enterprises, ASX-listed companies and the Australian Federal Government. We have secure, sovereign data centre campuses strategically located in Sydney and Canberra. Our flagship Macquarie Park Data Centre Campus , in Sydney’s north zone, is purpose-built to support the growing demands of AI and cloud in Australia’s digital economy.
We are serious about compliance and certification. We’re part of the nation’s critical infrastructure and are Certified Strategic by the Australian Federal Government.

Making a difference in our community.
For 30 years we have supported the communities we live and work in through United Way. We have proudly sponsored United Way campaigns for flood relief and bushfire appeals, participated in the Macquarie Business Park Community Walkathon and donated hundreds of books for kids under the age of 5.
We support our nearest neighbour East Timor, by funding young East Timorese to undergo life saving heart operations in Melbourne.
