Frequently Asked Questions.
What is a data centre?
Data centres serve as highly secure, dependable, and well-connected physical spaces to house physical IT infrastructure. They are utilized globally by organizations & governments to safeguard and house the world’s most vital data.
What are the key 5 elements of data centre?
A well-designed data centre encompasses 5 key elements ( amongst others) such as connectivity, power, cooling, security, and location. Here’s what you can expect from a top-notch data centre:
Efficient connectivity that enables seamless data exchange with external networks, cloud services, and other data centres.
Reliable power supply with multiple redundancies to ensure uninterrupted operation uptime.
Comprehensive cooling systems to maintain an optimal temperature for the infrastructure.
Robust security measures to protect against data compromise or theft.
Proximity to major CBD’s and innovation hubs to allow for low latency connections and processing
Together, these factors create a high-performing data centre that prioritizes efficiency, reliability, and security.
