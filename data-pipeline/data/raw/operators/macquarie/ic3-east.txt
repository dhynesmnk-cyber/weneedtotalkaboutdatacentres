Frequently Asked Questions.
What is a data centre?
A data centre is a secure, resilient and well-connected facility that houses critical IT infrastructure. It provides the power, cooling, connectivity, and security needed to store, process, and protect data while ensuring continuous availability and optimal performance. Data centres are essential for supporting everything from everyday online services to complex business, government, and AI workloads.
What types of data centres do you operate?
We operate hyperscale data centres for cloud providers and AI workloads, colocation facilities for enterprises and international organisations, and sovereign government-grade data centres that meet Australia’s strict security standards.
Where are your data centres located in Australia and Sydney?
We operate three data centre campuses in Sydney and Canberra , each housing multiple facilities designed to meet diverse customer needs. Our campuses provide scalable capacity, advanced cooling, high-density power, and robust security, delivering reliable infrastructure for cloud providers, government, enterprises, and AI workloads.
What are the specifications of your facilities?
We operate facilities equipped with high-density power, efficient hybrid, air and liquid cooling systems, and strong physical and cyber security. Designed for hyperscalers, government, enterprises, and AI, each data centre complies with Australia’s strict regulatory standards and international security and compliance frameworks.
For detailed technical specifications of each campus, please visit our Data Centres page.
What security measures do you have in place?
We maintain the highest levels of security across all our data centres through a multi-layered strategy that integrates physical protection, cutting-edge security systems, and expert personnel.
Here’s how we deliver the highest level of security for our customers:
6+ points of secure access including biometric scanning.
24/7/365 Physical and digital monitoring.
We have zones classified under the PSPF (SCEC) Zoning which identifies security zones (1 to 5) for the protection of government information and assets.
270+ Highly trained and government security-cleared staff. These security clearances are issued by the Australian
Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA).
Surveillance and Photography Low light surveillance cameras around the perimeter of each facility and internal CCTV
Surveillance cameras cover all entry points.
We own and operate a fully sovereign service.

Clients from industries such as technology, AI and machine learning, critical infrastructure , defence, high-performance computing, graphics processing, financial services, and healthcare trust us to protect and power their operations.

How do you ensure redundancy and reliability at your data centres?
We hold Australia’s longest running track record for 100% Uptime, 25 years and counting. We design all our data centres with redundancy and reliability as core principles. Our facilities hold Uptime Institute Tier certifications and use diverse, redundant cooling and power systems to maintain continuous operations.
What measures are taken by you to ensure the energy efficiency of their facilities?
Our facilities are built for energy efficiency from the ground up. Using best-in-class technology, advanced monitoring, and real-time analytics, we optimise power usage across all operations. This approach enables us to consistently maintain a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) below the industry average, reducing environmental impact while delivering high-performance infrastructure for our customers. For more information about our Environmental, Social and Governence (ESG) initiatves, visit our ESG page .
