MEL1 Melbourne

Where the cloud meets the ground in Melbourne.
Welcome to one of Australia’s largest hyperscale data centres (by megawatts).

AirTrunk acknowledges Traditional Owners of Country throughout Australia and recognises the continuing connection to lands, waters and communities.
We pay our respect to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultures; and to Elders past and present.

Key features

  • Location

    Located in Melbourne’s West and easily accessible to the CBD

  • Capacity

    A hyperscale campus with multiple diverse buildings and a total capacity of 185+MW

  • Size

    Set on 8.8ha, with 32,000m2 of technical data hall area, 12,600m2 of office and storage area and 34 data halls

  • Carrier Neutral

    Carrier neutral with multiple entry paths

  • Power

    66kV diverse high voltage power feed

  • Efficiency

    Design PUE of 1.15

  • Reliability

    100% availability backed by industry leading SLAs

  • Speed

    Quick deployment to meet demand

  • Certifications

    Designed for highest security and quality: Australian Government Certified Strategic hosting provider, SCEC Zone 3 or higher. Scope available upon request.
