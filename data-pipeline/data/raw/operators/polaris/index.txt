Polaris Data Centre is Queensland’s largest, 100% Australian owned data centre providing world-class hosting facilities to public and private organisations.
The facility is built to the highest standard, delivering an N+2, high availability solution that is delivered with 99.999% uptime. Polaris is carrier neutral providing tenants access to all major carriers as well as a 140km 720 core fibre ring into the Brisbane CBD via diverse paths.
Our Certifications
Facility highlights
Sovereign and certified
Polaris is 100% owned by the Springfield City Group, a 100% Queensland-owned business. Polaris is a Certified Strategic Provider under the Hosting Certification Framework (HCF), and holds certifications in ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, and ISO 45001 and is compliant with customer requirements for PCI DSS certification.
Largest Facility
in Queensland with over 14,500m 2 over 5 levels, with over 7,000m 2 of raised technical floor space, customers will always have space to grow.
Connected
Carrier neutral with presence of all major carriers, public cloud on-ramp (access via Megaport) and a 140km dark-fibre ring to the Brisbane CBD interconnecting to most major Brisbane Data Centres.
Flexible & Scaleable
Customised solutions from a simple rack structure to an entire secure floor, the facility is capable of modular fit-outs and installation of additional security, UPS, Chillers and Cooling, including direct and immersion liquid cooling.
Secure
24/7 manned security and facilities management all cleared by the Australian Federal Police and AGSVA, biometric person-traps, full security camera monitoring, supported on dual hardware infrastructure.
Location
Located in Springfield, 25km from Brisbane’s CBD and 19km from Amberly Airbase. Springfield is not susceptible to flooding or other natural disasters and is at low risk of geo-political events.
Sustainable
Providing green power options to all tenants in the facility and is a key tenant to Greater Springfields’ Net-Zero 2038 roadmap.
Remote services
Full remote hands offering including “smart eyes” video call services.
