Membership

Join the BCA

The Business Council of Australia represents a broad range of sectors committed to delivering a strong Australia that is globally competitive, productive, fair and inclusive.

Through the BCA, our members speak in a united voice backed by real-world examples and case studies that demonstrate the impact of proposed policy changes on the business and broader community.

Engagement with our members is targeted and outcomes-driven with a focus on leveraging member insights and experience to drive policy development, share best practice and solve problems.

  • Member driven policy advocacy

    Shape public policy supporting economic growth and social progress for the benefit of all Australians.

  • Connecting leaders

    Engage with governments, key decision makers and subject matter experts though our events and peer-to-peer networking program.

  • Global engagement

    Connect with international partners and share insights on global business trends, trade, investment and policy shifts.

  • Business advocacy

    Promote the role of business in creating jobs and driving national prosperity.
