M4 Melbourne Data Centre
In Planning
As part of the Fishermans Bend urban renewal precinct in Port Melbourne, M4 Melbourne is targeting 150MW of IT capacity. Currently In Planning, the facility will be a NVIDIA-certified AI Factory interconnected to other Melbourne facilities to deliver low-latency access to clouds AI and large scale enterprise platforms.
Built for a combination of hyperscale, neocloud, technology, enterprise and Government customers, M4 Melbourne will serve as a hub for high tech backed by comprehensive ISO, SOC and PCI-DSS and sustainability controls. Designed, built and operated to Tier IV standardsTier IV Gold for Operational Sustainability and NEXTDC’s 100% uptime guarantee. It provides sovereign, fault-tolerant infrastructure for mission-critical and regulated workloads.
