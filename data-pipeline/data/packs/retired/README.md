# Retired packs

Packs in this directory are **not loaded** by `scripts/rebuild_all.py` or the `Makefile`.
They are kept for provenance only.

| Pack | Retired | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `rg060_consent_conditions.json` | 2026-09-18 | Superseded by `rg060_consent_matrix.json`. This pack was **never wired into either pipeline**, so its 8 curated metrics silently never reached the database. 5 were independently regenerated by the matrix pack; the 3 unique ones (`consents_analysed_reliably`, `consents_extraction_limited`, `consents_with_generator_hours_cap`) were moved into `scripts/curate_rg060.py`, where they are **computed from source** rather than hardcoded. Found during the v2.0.0 consultancy-layer build. |

**Lesson recorded here so it is not repeated:** a curated pack that is not listed in
`scripts/rebuild_all.py` `PACKS` *and* the `Makefile` `PACKS`/`PACKS_ALLOW_MISSING`
variables does nothing at all. Adding a pack means wiring it in both places. The
consistency check between those two lists and `data/packs/` on disk should be part of
any review.
