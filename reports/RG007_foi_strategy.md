# RG-007 — FOI and disclosure strategy: do Australian data centres receive tax or land concessions?

**Status:** drafted, not lodged · **Prepared:** 18 September 2026 · **Priority 5**

## The question

The project brief asserts that state governments offer land tax exemptions and payroll tax waivers to
attract foreign hyperscalers. **No evidence for this has been located in Australia**, and the sector's
industry body asserts the opposite:

> "Data centres pay all standard taxes — corporate tax, payroll tax, land tax, stamp duty, council
> rates and GST. They receive no special tax concessions."
> — Data Centres Australia

Both positions are currently unverifiable from public sources. That is itself the finding, and it is
why this gap is priority 5: **the subsidy thesis in Pillar B cannot be asserted or refuted until
revenue-office records are obtained.**

What *is* documented is non-cash state support — fast-tracking, endorsement and coordination:

| Mechanism | Jurisdiction | Verified source |
|---|---|---|
| 75-day assessment for projects meeting 17 performance measures | NSW | NSW Data Centre Guidelines, 17 Aug 2026 |
| Investment Delivery Authority endorsement (15 projects, $51.9bn) | NSW | Ministerial release, 27 Mar 2026 |
| Approvals authority for projects above A$1bn | NSW | Pinsent Masons, 30 Sep 2025 |
| Fast-track reducing approvals to ~3 months | VIC | Guardian Australia, 26 Jul 2026 |
| Sustainable Data Centre Action Plan, A$5.5m | VIC | DJSIR / Invest Victoria |
| Priority consideration in Commonwealth regulatory processes | Cth | Expectations, 23 Mar 2026 |

And one documented flow **from** a developer **to** a council:

| Mechanism | Parties | Verified source |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary Planning Agreement, executed 7 June 2023, monetary contributions for open space and landscaping in the Mamre Road Precinct | Penrith City Council ↔ Microsoft Datacenter (Australia) Pty Ltd, Lot 2 DP 1271142, 769 Mamre Road Kemps Creek | Penrith City Council |

## Route selection

FOI is the correct instrument for revenue-office records because concessions are administered
case-by-case and are not published. Note the asymmetry: an FOI request to a revenue office about a
*named taxpayer* will almost certainly be refused under taxpayer confidentiality provisions. The
requests below are therefore framed to seek **policy instruments, determinations, guidelines and
aggregate counts** rather than individual taxpayer assessments — that framing is what makes them
arguably releasable, and it should be preserved exactly when lodged.

Statutory basis and fees:

| Jurisdiction | Act | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSW | *Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009* | **$30** application fee; processing at **$30/hour** | A 50% processing reduction may apply. Application is invalid until the fee is paid. |
| VIC | *Freedom of Information Act 1982* | application fee per agency schedule | Apply to the specific agency (SRO Victoria, DJSIR, DTP) |
| QLD | *Right to Information Act 2009* | application fee per schedule | Queensland Revenue Office |
| Cth | *Freedom of Information Act 1982* | no application fee for non-personal documents | FIRB/Treasury route — see RG-006 |

## Request templates

### 1. Revenue NSW (NSW payroll tax and land tax)

> **Agency:** Revenue NSW
> **Act:** Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009 (NSW)
>
> I seek access to the following government information held by Revenue NSW, for the period
> 1 January 2019 to date:
>
> 1. Any guideline, policy document, ruling, Commissioner's determination, practice note, internal
>    instruction or eligibility criterion under which a payroll tax exemption, reduction, deferral or
>    refund has been or may be granted in respect of a data centre, data storage facility, or a
>    development classified as "Data Storage" under the *Environmental Planning and Assessment
>    Regulation 2021*.
> 2. Any guideline, policy document, ruling, Commissioner's determination or practice note under which
>    land tax has been or may be exempted, reduced, deferred or refunded in respect of land used for a
>    data centre, including any treatment of such land as exempt under s.127 of the *Land Tax
>    Management Act 2005* (NSW) or any equivalent provision.
> 3. The **number** of entities, and the **aggregate value** of payroll tax and land tax concessions,
>    granted in each financial year from 2019-20 to 2025-26 in respect of facilities whose principal
>    activity is data storage or data processing. *I do not seek the identity of any taxpayer or any
>    assessment of an individual taxpayer.*
> 4. Any submission, advice or briefing prepared by Revenue NSW for the NSW Data Centre Consultation
>    Paper (2026) or the NSW Data Centre Guidelines (August 2026) concerning payroll tax, land tax or
>    duty treatment of data centres.
> 5. Any record of a data centre proponent having applied for, or been granted, a payroll tax or land
>    tax concession, expressed as counts and aggregate values only.

### 2. NSW Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure / Investment NSW

> 1. All Voluntary Planning Agreements entered into in connection with a development classified as
>    "Data Storage", from 1 January 2019 to date, including the executed agreement, the explanatory
>    note, the contribution amount and payment schedule, and the identity of the parties. *(VPAs are
>    public planning instruments; this request should not attract taxpayer confidentiality.)*
> 2. All records of the criteria applied by the Investment Delivery Authority in Rounds 1 and 2 of its
>    endorsement process for data centre projects, including the assessment framework, the scoring or

> 3. Any agreement, memorandum of understanding, letter of offer, incentive deed or land transaction
>    record between the State of New South Wales and a data centre proponent, including in the Western
>    Sydney Employment Area, the Mamre Road Precinct and the Western Sydney Aerotropolis, from
>    1 January 2019 to date.
> 4. Any record of land owned by the State, or by a State-owned corporation, that has been sold,
>    leased or optioned for data centre use, including the consideration and any concessional terms.

## Parallel routes that do not require FOI

These should be worked first because they are free, immediate and admissible:

1. **NSW Voluntary Planning Agreements register** — published; VPAs are public planning instruments.
   Survey every VPA in Blacktown, Penrith, Fairfield, Ryde, Lane Cove, Willoughby, Hills Shire, Camden
   and Cumberland where the development type is Data Storage. This yields the developer→council
   contribution total without any FOI. *(RG-025.)*
2. **State budget papers and Treasurer's determinations** — payroll tax and land tax threshold changes
   and any sector-specific measures are disclosed in budget paper No. 3 / revenue statements. Search
   2019-20 through 2026-27 for "data centre".
3. **NSW Data Centre Consultation Paper (March 2026) submissions** — the consultation ran for six weeks
   and submissions are generally published. Industry and union submissions frequently disclose whether
   concessions exist, because they lobby for or against them.
4. **NSW Legislative Council data centres inquiry submissions and hearings** — approximately 120+
   submissions, freely downloadable, with transcripts from the 29 May 2026 hearings. A direct question
   on tax concessions put to a revenue officer in a public hearing is the fastest possible answer.
   *(RG-015.)*
5. **IPART water pricing review for data centres** — commissioned 17 August 2026. Its issues paper and
   submissions will disclose whether data centres pay full long-run marginal cost for water or are
   cross-subsidised, which is the water analogue of the subsidy question.
6. **ASIC and ABR** — free/paid extracts resolve SPV ownership, which establishes whether the ultimate
   parent is foreign and therefore whether any concession would be a foreign-investor concession.
   *(RG-021, RG-029, RG-035.)*

## Expected outcomes and how to record them

| Outcome | Recording |
|---|---|
| A concession instrument exists | `incentives` row with `incentive_type` = payroll_tax_exemption / land_tax_exemption / leasehold_land_concession, `instrument` = the document, `conditionality_score`, `disclosed` = 1, `fact_status` = VERIFIED |
| No concession instrument exists | `incentives` row with `incentive_type` = none_found, `fact_status` = VERIFIED, `notes` recording the FOI reference and the agency's response date. **This is a publishable negative finding and settles the Pillar B question in the sector's favour.** |
| Refusal on taxpayer confidentiality | `incentives` row with `fact_status` = GAP, and a `research_gaps` entry recording the refusal and the review route (NSW Information and Privacy Commission; VIC Ombudsman/VCAT; Cth OAIC) |
| Refusal on the ground that no such information exists | Treat as the second case: a verified negative |

Either way the gap closes. **A documented negative is as valuable as a documented positive here**, and
the Observatory's credibility depends on recording it neutrally rather than treating the absence of
evidence as evidence of concealment.

## Effort estimate

Five requests at $30 each in NSW ($150), plus Victorian and Queensland fees. NSW statutory decision
period is 20 working days, extendable; assume 8-12 weeks to first responses and longer if review is
needed. Routes 1-4 above are free and can be worked immediately — route 4 in particular may answer
the question from an existing public transcript before any FOI is decided.
